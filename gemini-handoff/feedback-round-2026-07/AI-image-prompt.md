**How to use this file**: one prompt per block, separated by `-------------`.
For every prompt, attach the reference images named in that block (they are in
THIS folder) before pasting the text. Ask for **landscape 16:9, at least
1440 × 803 px, JPEG**. These are editorial photographs for articles — no brand
characters, no logos, and **no readable text anywhere in the image** (signage,
posters, packaging must stay blurred or blank).

**Reject and re-run if**: any readable text or watermark appears, hands have
wrong finger counts, faces look plastic or over-smoothed, the light turns cold
or blue-grey, or a toy in frame resembles Lumi or Kheelu (these photos must
never look like product shots).

**Style, for every prompt**: match the two reference photos in this folder —
`ref-style-grandmother-moon.jpg` and `ref-style-father-kites.jpg`. Warm
golden-hour or soft indoor evening light, an Indian family in an ordinary
middle-class Indian home, natural candid documentary feel, gentle depth of
field, nothing staged or stocky.
