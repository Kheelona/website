Why we exist

Your child's best years deserve more than a screen.

Between the ages of 3 and 6, a child's brain builds faster than it ever will again. What they hear, what they feel, and who they talk to shape everything that follows.

Most toys do one thing. Screens do too much. Lumi does the one thing that matters: it listens to your child, understands how they feel, and talks back in a way that helps them grow.

Meet the feelings

Learning starts with feeling understood.

Lumi knows five feelings. They are the engine behind everything your child learns.

Curious

Asks why. Chases ideas. Wants to know what is around the corner.

Grumpy

Has opinions. Not always wrong. Needs to be heard, not hushed.

Sad

Sits with you. Does not rush past. Makes space for the hard moments.

Silly

Cannot sit still. Turns everything into a game. Laughter is learning too.

Joy

Lights up. Celebrates. Reminds your child that they are wonderful.

The technology behind the talking.

Lumi runs on PlayOS, Kheelona's own voice engine built from the ground up for children aged 3 to 6. One platform. One set of safety rules. Every product in the family runs on the same soul.

01

Think about the device first.

When your child speaks, PlayOS processes the conversation on the device before anything goes anywhere. It reads the feeling behind the words, not just the words. Low latency. No long waits. No sending everything to a distant server.

02

Every response is the right response.

Before Lumi speaks, every response passes through an age-graded safety layer. On-device and cloud filters work together. No open internet. No random content. No surprises. PlayOS is what makes Lumi a cognitive development toy, not just a toy that plays sounds.
