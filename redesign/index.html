Questions

Ask away. We’re all ears.

What is Kheelu?

Kheelu by Kheelona is a screen-free plush rabbit that talks with children aged 3 to 7. It answers your child, then asks a question back. It has three modes: AI mode for open conversation on home Wi‑Fi, Story mode for stories, poems and jingles with no Wi‑Fi needed, and Bluetooth mode, which makes it a speaker for your phone.

What if my child doesn’t like it?

Return it within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked. Opened is fine: just send back everything that came in the box, undamaged. We arrange the pickup, free. Once it reaches us and qualifies for return, your refund reaches you within 7 working days. Until we dispatch it, everything you’ve paid is refundable in full.

How much is Kheelu, and how does the advance work?

Kheelu is ₹4,999 in total, with delivery anywhere in India included. A refundable ₹499 reserves yours today, and the rest is due before delivery, by a payment link we send you. You can pay by UPI, card or net banking.

Isn’t this just screen time in disguise?

No. There’s no screen to watch: your child talks, listens and does the thinking. Each Kheelu has a daily limit on how long it talks, and in the parent app you can see how long your child talked each day.

Won’t an AI toy make my child think less?

That’s exactly what Kheelu is designed against. Most AI gives answers. Kheelu answers your child, then asks them a question back, so they do the thinking. It can’t browse the internet, and when it doesn’t know something, it says so instead of guessing. You can read every conversation in the parent app.

What ages is Kheelu for?

Kheelu is made for children aged 3 to 7. It is not a toy for children under 3. If your child is 3, keep a grown-up nearby. At setup, you enter your child’s age in the parent app. You can change it later. The AI model gets child-safety rules for that age, with Google’s safety filters on top.

When will my Kheelu arrive?

Deliveries start mid-November. Pre-orders are served first, in the order they were placed, and you get a tracking link when it leaves us. Delivery is included anywhere in India, with nothing to pay at the door. We don’t ship outside India. A reserved Kheelu is sent once the rest of the price is paid.

Is Kheelu the same as Lumi?

Yes. Kheelu is the toy that was called Lumi until 5 September 2026: the same product from Kheelona, with a new name. You may still see the old name on some older listings. A toy sold as Lumi by any other company is a different product, and Kheelona does not make it.
