How it works

A toy that talks back, not a toy that repeats.

Khelu is a screen-free plush toy for ages 3 to 7 that holds a back-and-forth conversation. It answers your child, then asks a question back, so they do the thinking.

AI mode

Open conversation, on your home Wi‑Fi. It can’t browse the internet.

✦

Story mode

Stories, poems and jingles your child enjoys and learns from. No Wi‑Fi needed.

Bluetooth mode

Pair your phone and use it as a speaker.

Good to know

  • AI mode needs your home Wi‑Fi. Story mode and Bluetooth mode work without it.

  • It can’t browse the internet. When it doesn’t know something, it says so instead of guessing.

  • Each conversation starts with a press of the wake-up button. It switches itself off after a couple of minutes of quiet.

  • Between conversations nothing is sent anywhere, and when Khelu rests, its Wi‑Fi is switched off.

  • Each Khelu has a daily limit on how long it talks.

  • At setup, you enter your child’s age in the parent app.

  • It charges with the charger in the box.

Questions

Questions parents ask.

Still have a question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Will Khelu do the thinking for my child?

No. That is what it is built against. Most AI gives answers. Khelu answers your child, then asks a question back, so they do the thinking. When it doesn’t know something, it says so instead of guessing. It can’t browse the internet, and you can read every conversation in the parent app.

What is the difference between a talking toy and a toy that talks back?

Many talking toys play recorded phrases or repeat what your child says. A toy that talks back replies to what your child says, so a conversation can go back and forth. Khelu is a screen-free plush toy for ages 3 to 7 that answers your child, then asks a question back.

Is Khelu right for a 3‑year‑old?

Khelu is made for children aged 3 to 7, and it is not a toy for children under 3. If your child is 3, keep a grown-up nearby. At setup, you enter your child’s age in the parent app. You can change it later. The AI model gets child-safety rules for that age, with Google’s safety filters on top.

What does Khelu cost, and what is the advance for?

Khelu is ₹4,999 in total, with delivery anywhere in India included. The ₹499 is a refundable advance that reserves yours; the rest is due before delivery. Pay by UPI, card or net banking, with no payment fees.

Isn’t this just screen time in disguise?

No. There’s no screen to watch: your child talks, listens and does the thinking. Each Khelu has a daily limit on how long it talks, and in the parent app you can see how long your child talked each day.

What if my child doesn’t like it, or it stops working?

Return it within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked; opened is fine if it comes back undamaged and complete. Pickup is free. Arrived faulty? Tell us within 7 days for a replacement or a full refund. The 6-month warranty covers manufacturing defects, not drops, water damage or misuse: we repair it, or replace it if the repair doesn’t work.

Will my child still play with it after the first week?

No toy can promise that. Khelu remembers earlier conversations, so it can pick up where your child stopped instead of starting over. Story mode adds stories, poems and jingles, with no Wi‑Fi needed. You can read any conversation in the parent app.

Will Kheelona+ charge me after the 6 months?

No, not unless you sign up for it yourself. Every Khelu comes with 6 months of Kheelona+, our paid subscription, on top of the free parent app. Nothing renews without you.

Which languages does Khelu speak?

Khelu speaks up to 10 languages at launch, including English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Spanish and French. In AI mode, it can switch between them mid-sentence: when your child changes language, Khelu changes with them. You can watch it happen in the film “Two languages”.

Can Khelu help my child’s speech?

Khelu is a toy, not a therapy or treatment. Harvard’s Center on the Developing Child says back-and-forth “serve and return” exchanges between a young child and a caring adult support early language and social skills.[1] Khelu doesn’t replace that time with you. If you’re worried about your child’s speech, talk to your paediatrician.
