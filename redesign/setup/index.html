Day one with Khelu

How to set up Khelu on day one.

Charge Khelu, then open the parent app on your phone. The app connects Khelu to your home Wi‑Fi and asks for your child’s age. Then show your child the wake-up button: one press starts a conversation. Afterwards, you can read every word of it in the app.

Before you start.

  • Charge Khelu with the charger in the box.

  • Have your home Wi‑Fi password ready.

  • Stay close to your Wi‑Fi router.

  • Keep your phone with you, with the parent app installed. It’s called the Kheelona app: get it on the App Store for iPhone, or on Google Play for Android.

Khelu connects to 2.4 GHz Wi‑Fi. If your router shows two network names, choose the 2.4 GHz one.

Connect Khelu to your home Wi‑Fi.

  1. Open the parent app.

    Follow the steps on screen. The app walks you through the rest.

  2. Press and hold the button on Khelu’s back.

    Hold it for 3 seconds, until its light starts pulsing.

  3. Join Khelu’s own Wi‑Fi.

    When the app asks, connect your phone to Khelu’s own small Wi‑Fi network.

  4. Add your home Wi‑Fi.

    Finish in the app with your home Wi‑Fi password. Khelu then joins your home network.

Tell the app your child’s age.

You enter your child’s age in the parent app. On every conversation, the AI model that answers your child is given that age and child-safety rules, with Google’s safety filters on top.

You can change it later. Khelu picks up the change the next time it’s online.

Khelu is made for children aged 3 to 7. If your child is 3, keep a grown-up nearby.

While you’re in the app, you can also choose your family’s languages.

Show your child the wake-up button.

Khelu only listens when someone presses its wake-up button. It’s the button on Khelu’s back, the one you held during setup.

One press starts a conversation. A light on Khelu shows whenever it’s listening. After a couple of minutes of quiet, it switches itself off, so the next chat starts with another press.

Then let them talk.

The first conversation is your child’s. Khelu answers, then asks a question back.

Later, open the parent app to read every word of it.

No Wi‑Fi for a while? Story mode’s stories, poems and jingles still play. AI mode needs your home Wi‑Fi, and Bluetooth mode needs only your paired phone.
