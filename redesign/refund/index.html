Legal

Refunds, returns and warranty, in plain words.

Khelu’s refund policy: you can change your mind. This page explains exactly how, and what happens to your money.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

KheluChanged your mind? That’s allowed.

The short version.

  • Before dispatch: a full refund.

    Cancel any time before your Khelu leaves us and get back everything you’ve paid. No fee, no reason needed.

  • After delivery: 7 days to return.

    No questions asked. Opened is fine, if everything comes back undamaged. We arrange the pickup, free. You get a full refund of everything you paid.

  • When your money comes back.

    After a cancellation, we start your refund within 2 working days. Then your bank takes 5-7 working days. After a return, your refund reaches you within 7 working days of your Khelu reaching us.

  • Faulty or not as described?

    You can have a replacement or a full refund.

  • 6-month warranty.

    On manufacturing defects. We repair it, or replace it if the repair doesn’t work.

  • No exchanges. No payment fees.

    Nothing recurring, and nothing is ever charged automatically.

To cancel, return or claim: call or message us on WhatsApp at +91 91875 46483, or email hello@kheelona.com, with your order number.

1. Cancelling before dispatch.

Everything you’ve paid to pre-order is refundable in full at any time before your Khelu is dispatched: the refundable ₹499 advance, or the whole price if you paid in full. There is no fee, no deduction, and no reason you have to give.

To cancel, call or message us on WhatsApp at +91 91875 46483, or email hello@kheelona.com, with your order number (it starts with KH-). A message or an email is enough: you never have to call. We start your refund within 2 working days of your request; your bank takes 5-7 working days.

If you don’t pay the balance

On a reservation, the remaining ₹4,500 of the ₹4,999 price (incl. GST) is due before delivery, by a payment link we send on WhatsApp and by email. Not paying it counts as a cancellation, and we treat it exactly like one: we ask you twice over two weeks, and if we don’t hear from you, we release your Khelu and refund your ₹499 in full. Nothing is ever charged automatically.

If the date moves, or we can’t deliver

Deliveries start mid-November. If that date moves, you hear it from us first, and you can cancel for a full refund, even if you were happy to wait before. If we can’t supply your Khelu for any reason, you get back everything you’ve paid: the money, not a credit or a voucher.

2. 7-day returns, no questions asked.

Changed your mind? Return your Khelu within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked. Opened is fine. Just send back everything that came in the box, undamaged: the plush toy, the charger and the quick-start card, in their box.

How a return works

  1. Call or message us on WhatsApp, or email us, within 7 days of delivery, with your order number.
  2. We arrange the pickup from your address, free.
  3. Once your Khelu reaches us and qualifies for return, your refund reaches you within 7 working days. It’s a full refund of everything you paid.

If your Khelu comes back with something missing, or damaged beyond normal play, we’ll talk to you before deciding the refund.

3. Faulty, damaged or not as described.

Faulty or not as described? You can have a replacement or a full refund.

In the first 7 days

If your Khelu arrives damaged or faulty, or isn’t what we described, tell us within 7 days of receiving it, and you can have a replacement or a full refund. The conditions in section 2 (undamaged, complete, in the box) are for change-of-mind returns only: they don’t apply to a faulty, damaged or not-as-described Khelu.

The 6-month warranty

Khelu has a 6-month warranty on manufacturing defects, from the day it’s delivered. If a defect shows up after the first 7 days, we repair it, or replace it if the repair doesn’t work. The warranty doesn’t cover drops, water damage or misuse.

Pickup and timing

Pickup is free for every faulty unit and every warranty claim. Sending the repaired or replacement Khelu back to you is free too. How long a repair or a replacement takes depends on where in India you are, so we don’t promise a number of days. To claim, call or message us on WhatsApp, or email us, with your order number and what’s wrong.

4. Exchanges.

No exchanges: return it within 7 days and order again. If your Khelu is faulty or not as described, that isn’t an exchange: section 3 gives you a replacement or a full refund.

5. How and when your money comes back.

Every refund goes back to the card, UPI ID or bank account you paid from, because that’s the only place a payment can be reversed to.

  • Cancelled before dispatch: we start your refund within 2 working days of your request; your bank takes 5-7 working days.
  • Returned, faulty or not as described: your refund reaches you within 7 working days of your Khelu reaching us.

6. Payments, fees and chargebacks.

No payment fees. Nothing recurring. Payments by Razorpay.

You can pay by UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking. Prices include GST, and every order comes with a GST invoice. Your card number, UPI ID and bank details go to Razorpay, not to us, and nothing is ever charged automatically.

If a payment goes wrong, call or message us with your order number. You can also raise a dispute (a chargeback) with your bank or card issuer. Razorpay’s own customer support is at razorpay.com/support: choose “Customer”.

7. Complaints.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled a refund, a return or a repair, you can make a formal complaint to our grievance officer.

Grievance Officer: Aman Soni, Co-Founder and CTO, aman@kheelona.com, +91 91875 46483. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month of receiving it.
