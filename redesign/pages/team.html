Why we build Khelu.

We are parents who build. We watched our own children reach for screens.

Most AI gives children answers. We built a toy that answers, then asks your child a question back, so they do the thinking.

And you hold the keys. You can read every conversation in the parent app, and “Delete all chats” there deletes them from our servers. You set your child’s age, and Khelu’s AI is given child-safety rules and that age on every conversation.

No screen. No camera. It can’t browse the internet.
