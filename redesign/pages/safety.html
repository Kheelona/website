Are AI toys safe? Check ours, fact by fact.

Not all of them, so check before you buy one for a child aged 3 to 7. Ask when its microphone is on, how it understands your child, whether it can browse the internet, and whether you can read every conversation. Here’s how Khelu answers each one, in plain words.

Four facts

Is Khelu safe for my child?

Judge it by what it does. Here are the four facts that matter most, in full.

Is Khelu always listening?

No. Khelu has no wake word to listen for. Its microphone turns on only when someone presses the wake-up button. It stays on while your child is talking and switches itself off after a couple of minutes of quiet. Until the next press, nothing is heard, recorded or sent. A light on Khelu shows whenever it’s listening.

How does Khelu understand and answer my child?

To understand and answer your child, Khelu uses trusted speech and AI providers. They process your child’s voice only to produce Khelu’s reply. They don’t keep it, and they never use it to train their AI. Our contracts with them say so.

Can I read every conversation?

Yes, in the parent app, word for word, a few minutes after each one ends. “Delete all chats” in the app deletes them from our servers.

Do you sell my child’s data?

No. We don’t sell your data, and your child’s data is never used to advertise to them.

Questions

Questions parents ask.

Is Khelu OK for a 3‑year‑old?

Khelu is made for children aged 3 to 7, and it is not a toy for children under 3. If your child is 3, keep a grown-up nearby. Common Sense Media advises against AI toys at this age. Its advice in full, and our answer, are in the next question.

What do child-safety groups say about AI toys?

In January 2026, Common Sense Media advised parents not to give AI toys, which it describes as “smart toys with voice-based interactions”, to children aged 5 and under, and to use extreme caution before buying them for children aged 6 to 12.1 We make Khelu for ages 3 to 7, so you should hear this from us.

When it tested three AI toys, it rated 27% of their replies as not appropriate for kids. It also raised concerns about toys designed to create emotional attachment, the data they collect and how reliably they work.2

Here is what Khelu does about each concern:

  • Replies: its answers are generated by an AI model that is given child-safety rules and your child’s age on every conversation, with Google’s safety filters on top. It can’t browse or search the web. When it doesn’t know something, it says so. You can read every conversation.
  • Attachment: Khelu tells your child it’s a toy, and it never asks them to keep a secret from you.
  • Data: the four facts at the top of this page. We don’t sell your data.
  • How well it works: if it isn’t right for your family, return it within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked.
  • At 3: keep a grown-up nearby.
