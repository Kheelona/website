# FeelingChip

Kheelu's five feelings (Curious, Grumpy, Sad, Silly, Joy) as a compact chip or a card with its description.

**Use it for:** the feelings section, story filters in the app, and social posts about emotional learning.

**Props:** `feeling` (`"curious"` | `"grumpy"` | `"sad"` | `"silly"` | `"joy"`), `card` (show the description), `tone` (colours the face dot with a brand fill; default cream), `label` and `description` (overrides; keep the site's lines by default).

**Rules**
- The chip is white with a cream face dot, so the five feelings are told apart by their faces and words, never by colour alone. In a themed layout, pass one `tone` to colour the dot. Kheelu's rainbow stays on the product itself (photos and the rainbow-arch shape) and is not a UI colour.
- Show all five together when explaining the idea, and one at a time in context.
- Grumpy and Sad are good feelings to name, not problems to fix. Never frame them as "bad".
