# ImaginationFrame

Kheelona's signature: a child with chalk (or ink) doodles of the idea they are thinking about, and their question handwritten above.

**Use it for:** learning and curiosity stories (hero blocks, social posts, decks). It shows Kheelu's intelligence through the child, never through tech imagery.

**Props:** `variant` (`"board"`: a cut-out child on the ink chalkboard with chalk doodles; `"photo"`: a full photo, used with `ink` for light photos), `cutout` or `photo` (asset URL), `cutoutStyle`, `alt`, `question` (lower case, the child's words), `questionAlign` (`"center"` | `"left"`), `doodles` (an array of `{ src, x, y, w, rotate?, clip? }` in % of the frame; `clip` is a CSS `clip-path` in % of the doodle that hides the part passing behind something in front, such as Kheelu in the child's arms), `tone` (block colour for non-board frames).

**Rules**
- Chalk on the board or on dark photos, ink on light photos. Lines must hold 3:1 against what's behind them.
- Use 3–5 doodles with one hero (helmet, crown, thought cloud). Never cross the eyes, nose or mouth.
- The helmet is worn: size it so the bubble holds the whole head, from above the hair to below the chin, with the ear pods at the ears and the collar at the neck. A crown sits on the hair.
- If the child holds Kheelu in front, `clip` the doodle so it goes behind Kheelu. `HomePageTrust` shows the helmet on `kabir-01-imagining-space`.
- Draw the topic (space, mangoes, trains, numbers), never the AI (chips, brains, sparkles, waves).
- Use the Doodles group only. New doodles must match the style (see **Photography, chalk & crayon**).
