# DeckSlide

A 16:9 slide in three layouts: **title** (headline left, cast and Kheelu stickers on a brand-colour block right), **statement** (one sentence, often on the chalkboard) and **facts** (three numbers).

**Use it for:** sales, partner, school and investor decks, and webinar and video title cards.

**Props:** `layout` (`"title"` | `"statement"` | `"facts"`), `tone`, `board` (chalkboard background), `eyebrow`, `title`, `hand`, `sub`, `kid`, `product`, `facts` (`{ num, label, tone? }`, up to 3), `foot`.

**Rules**
- One idea per slide and 30 words at most. Numbers come from approved facts only.
- Put statement slides on the board, with the `hand` phrase in `sun-500`. The footer carries the logo (Day, or Bedtime on the board).
- It always renders in the Day theme. Export at 1920×1080.
