# HomePagePhone

The home page on a phone, in three frames: the hero, the page scrolled with the sticky buy bar, and the menu open. A showcase page, not a bundle export.

**Use it for:** checking phone layouts. Most Indian parents arrive on a phone, so design this first.

**Props:** none. Open the preview to copy its markup.

**Sections:** `AnnouncementBar`, `NavBar` (cta and menu button) and the hero with a full-width cta and the `kh-art` board under the text · how it works with `StickyBuyBar` · `NavDrawer` over the scrim.

**Assets:** the same hero board as **HomePage**.

**Rules**
- The cta is full width; the trust line wraps; the art sits under the words.
- `StickyBuyBar` appears once the hero scrolls away and stays until the offer card is on screen.
- The `kh-device` frame is for showing screens in docs and decks; it is not part of the page.
