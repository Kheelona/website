# HomePage

The top of the Kheelona home page, built only from the system: the announcement bar, the nav, the hero with Myra and Kheelu on a Kheelu Blue block, the languages, how it works, year by year, and a day with Kheelu. A showcase page, not a bundle export: part 1 of 3, with **HomePageTrust** and **HomePageBuy**.

**Use it for:** starting any web page, landing page or campaign page. Copy the structure; swap the words, photos and stickers.

**Props:** none. Open the preview to copy its markup.

**Sections:** `AnnouncementBar` · `NavBar` · hero (`SectionHeading` hero, cta, `kh-trustline`, a `kh-art` board) with `LanguageChips` under it · how it works (`StepLoop`, band) · year by year (`AgeTimeline`) · a day with Kheelu (three `kh-photo` frames and `ModeSwitch`, band).

**Assets:** `myra-sticker.png`, `kheelu-front-sticker.png`, `sun-sun.svg`, `chalk-question-mark.svg`; `myra-01-breakfast-why`, `advik-01-counting-carriages`, `ira-01-story-on-the-bed` (web versions).

**Rules**
- One hero per page, one `cta` per view, and one `Hand` or `Mark` per headline.
- Bands alternate `surface` and `surface-band` (`kh-section--band`); each band has one colour moment.
- The hero board follows the scrapbook rule: one brand block, the child and Kheelu, one shape behind them, one doodle, one sticker.
- The children are illustrative. Captions describe the moment, never a customer.
