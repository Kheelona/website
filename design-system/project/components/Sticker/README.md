# Sticker

A tilted, white-outlined label that feels stuck on by hand: ages, offers, badges and short shouts.

**Use it for:** "Ages 3+", "First 500 only", "Screen-free", "Up to 10 languages", an age on a card, or a round price burst ("₹499 reserves yours").

**Props:** `tone` (`"sun"` | `"chilli"` | `"blue"`; default sun), `tilt` (degrees, default −3), `icon`, `hand` (Kalam lettering), `round` + `big` (round burst with a big number), children (1–4 words).

**Rules**
- Stickers use the brand fills: Sunshine (`sun-500`, the default) with `ink-900` text, and Chilli (`chilli-500`) and Kheelu Blue (`blue-500`) with white text. White on Chilli (2.9:1) and on Kheelu Blue (3.0:1) are brand decisions below WCAG AA, so keep chilli and blue stickers to a few big words and use Sunshine for anything people must read.
- Use at most two stickers per view, at different tilts (one negative, one positive, between −8° and +6°).
- Stickers are claims. Only use words from the approved facts ("Ships 20 Oct 2026", not "Fastest delivery").
- Don't put a sticker on top of a face or over Kheelu's belly unit.
