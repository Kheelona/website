# AgeTimeline

Four cream cards that show what Kheelu does at age 3, 4, 5 and every year after, each with an age sticker and a handwritten note.

**Use it for:** the "year by year" section, the "grows with them" proof in decks, and the age-fit FAQ.

**Props:** `items` (an array of `{ age, title, body, note?, tone? }`). Tones default to chilli, sun, blue, sun.

**Rules**
- Titles come from the site ("Asking why, and why again.", "Playing with ideas.", "Words, numbers, confidence.", "Growing right alongside.").
- The handwritten note is 2–4 words in the child's spirit ("asking is worth it"), never a claim.
- When you add a photo, match the age to the cast: Myra (3), Advik (4), Ira (5), Kabir (6).
