# Kheelu

Real product photography of Kheelu. Use these and never an AI or drawn version of the toy.

- `kheelu-front.png`: the master front cut-out, transparent background, 1080×1324. Use it for composites, web and decks.
- `kheelu-front.webp`: the same, compressed (175 KB) for websites.
- `kheelu-front-sticker.png`: with a white sticker outline and soft shadow, for crayon blocks, `SocialPost` and `DeckSlide`.
- `kheelu-on-fur.jpg`: 1600×1600 on `fur-100` with a contact shadow, for listings, WhatsApp, email and OG images. Make a pure-white version when a marketplace requires it.
- `kheelu-belly-module.png`: a crop of the belly unit (speaker, light ring, buttons), for setup guides and "how it works".

Check every use against the anatomy table in **Kheelu, the product**. Still needed: back and three-quarter views, an in-hand scale shot, the light ring lit and unlit, the packaging and the quick-start card.
