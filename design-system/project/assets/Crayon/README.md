# Crayon

Textured crayon marks for one word or number, in the three brand colours (Chilli #EF762F, Kheelu Blue #29A0D7, Sunshine #FFCF4D), with the colour baked in:

- `highlight-sun.svg` (`sun-500`): a marker swipe behind a headline word.
- `underline-chilli.svg`, `underline-blue.svg`, `underline-sun.svg`: a single underline (the sunshine one is double).
- `circle-chilli.svg`, `circle-blue.svg`: a loop around a price or number.
- `squiggle-blue.svg` (`blue-500`), `zigzag-chilli.svg` (`chilli-500`): edge decoration on social posts.
- `scribble-sun.svg`: a playful corner for kid-facing pieces.
- `burst-sun.svg`: sun rays behind the product on a plain ground.

In code, use `Kheelona.Mark` (tone `chilli`, `blue` or `sun`) instead of these files. Use one mark per headline and never under body copy. Chilli marks circle prices and offers; blue and sunshine marks are for everything else.
