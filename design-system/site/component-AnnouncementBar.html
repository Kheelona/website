AnnouncementBar

Open on its own ↗

A slim sun-yellow strip above the nav for the offer and the ship date.

Use it for: the current offer ("First 500 Kheelus at ₹4,999"), ship dates and one link.

Props: items (1–3 short strings, separated by dots), link ({ href, label }).

Rules

  • Keep it to one line on desktop. On phones it may wrap to two.
  • Use only real, current facts. Take it down the day the offer ends.
  • Don't put a chilli button in it. The link is underlined ink.
