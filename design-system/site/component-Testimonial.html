Testimonial

Open on its own ↗

A cream quote card with a handwritten quote mark, an initials avatar, and the name and role.

Use it for: real quotes from real pilot parents and customers, with their consent.

Props: quote (or children), name (first name only), role (default "Pilot parent"), city (optional), tone.

Rules

  • Only real people, real words (light trimming is fine; changing meaning is not), and written consent.
  • Never use cast photos or AI faces with a testimonial. Initials only, unless the parent provided their own photo for this use.
  • Show three at most at once, in different tones.
