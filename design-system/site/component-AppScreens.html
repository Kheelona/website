AppScreens

The parent app: setup, today, conversations, and settings in the Bedtime theme

Open on its own ↗

Four parent-app screens in device frames: setup, today, conversations, and settings in the Bedtime theme. A showcase page, not a bundle export.

Use it for: app design, onboarding flows, and app screenshots in decks and on the website.

Props: none. Open the preview to copy its markup.

Sections: setup (ProgressBar, TextField, Select, RadioGroup, a primary button) · today (Avatar, Alert, AppSummaryCard, TabBar) · conversations (Tabs, List and ListItems) · settings with data-theme="dark" (ModeSwitch, ParentControl, a primary button).

Assets: tara-portrait-web.webp for the child's avatar.

Rules

  • Everyday actions are Kheelu Blue primary with white text; Chilli never appears in the app except to buy.
  • Data screens have no doodles, stickers or crayon marks.
  • On phones, drop the mode icons so the three modes fit on one row.
