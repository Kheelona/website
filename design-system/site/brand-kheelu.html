Kheelu is the product we sell and the friend children love. It must look exactly like the real toy in every picture.

Anatomy: check every image against this

Part What it looks like
Body Cream plush rabbit, seated upright, soft long-pile fur
Ears Long and floppy, hanging to the shoulders, with a rainbow tie-dye lining (pink, peach, butter yellow, mint, sky, lilac)
Feet Large round foot pads in the same rainbow tie-dye
Face Small black bead eyes set wide; a grey, slightly sparkly triangular nose; a stitched mouth; no eyebrows and no glasses on the toy
Belly unit A round, light-grey hardware disc in the tummy: a dotted speaker grille in a fan pattern, a soft blue light ring around the edge, and a row of five buttons (−, previous, play/pause, next, +), and below them a + button, a power button and one more function button

If any of these is missing, distorted, recoloured or added to (glasses, clothes, a screen, eyes that glow, a mouth that opens), the image is rejected. Fix it by compositing the real photo from the Kheelu group, not by regenerating.

Still needed (please add when available): back view, three-quarter view, the toy held by a child for scale, a lit/unlit pair of the light ring, the packaging and the quick-start card. Real photography is preferred over AI for all of these.

How to show Kheelu

  • Whole and upright. Never crop through the face or the belly unit. Ears may touch the frame edge.
  • The belly unit is visible in every hero shot, because it is the proof that this plush is also a talking friend.
  • Real scale. Next to a 3–6 year old, Kheelu sits on a lap or in arms, at roughly the size of the child's torso. Never shrink it to a keychain or enlarge it to child size.
  • Warm light, cream fur. Keep the fur cream, never grey, white or yellow. The rainbow stays soft pastel, never neon. It is the product's colour, not the brand's: never pull it into layouts beyond the rainbow-arch shape. It is not a UI colour or a token.
  • One Kheelu per frame. No product-army grids, except a single family shot of the product line when the other products launch.
  • On colour: use the sticker version on brand-colour blocks, and kheelu-on-fur.jpg or the plain cut-out on fur grounds. Use shadow-lift only when it floats on a plain ground.
  • Never add a screen, glowing eyes, holograms, speech bubbles coming from the belly with fake text, or sparkles around Kheelu. Kheelu speaks through the child's reaction and the Imagination layer.

Kheelu's five feelings

Kheelu acts out five feelings so children learn to name their own. Each has its own face icon and line, through FeelingChip. The chip stays white with a cream face dot, so the feelings are told apart by face and word, never by colour alone.

Feeling Icon Line (from the site)
Curious question Asks why. Chases ideas. Wants to know what is around the corner.
Grumpy smiley-angry Names the hard feeling instead of hiding it.
Sad smiley-sad Sits with you. Does not rush past. Makes space for the hard moments.
Silly smiley-wink Turns a dull afternoon into a game.
Joy smiley Celebrates the small wins out loud.

The illustrated Kheelu mascot (the winking character on kheelona.com) is not in this system yet. When its files are added, keep it to these five expressions. Never redraw it.

What Kheelu does (the approved facts)

  • A screen-free AI friend for ages 3+ that listens, remembers and grows with the child.
  • Three modes: AI mode on home Wi-Fi, Story mode that works offline, and Bluetooth mode for your own music.
  • Up to 10 languages at launch; English, Hindi, Bengali, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Spanish and French announced.
  • Wake-word listening: the microphone is off until the wake word, and nothing is recorded before it.
  • First safety filtering on the device. Answers come from a closed, child-focused library, with no open internet.
  • Parents read every conversation, delete any of it, and set topics, quiet hours and languages in the parent app.
  • Data is region-pinned and parent-consented, and is never sold.
  • In the box: Kheelu, a charger and a quick-start card. Full specs (battery, size, materials, wake word) are published before shipping; don't guess them.
