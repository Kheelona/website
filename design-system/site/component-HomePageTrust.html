The middle of the home page, where trust is earned: what Kheelu does, the Imagination layer, safety, the five feelings and the parent app. A showcase page, not a bundle export: part 2 of 3.

Use it for: product, safety and parent-app pages, and any long page that needs proof before the price.

Props: none. Open the preview to copy its markup.

Sections: three FeatureCards (plain, tint, block) · ImaginationFrame with chalk doodles beside a SectionHeading (band) · TrustList, the secondary and cta buttons and the "Recognised by" line · a kh-photo with FeelingChips (band) · the parent app: a kh-photo and AppSummaryCard with TabBar in a kh-device frame.

Assets: kabir-01-imagining-space with chalk-astronaut-helmet, chalk-rocket, chalk-planet and chalk-star; tara-01-naming-feelings; tara-02-evening-with-amma.

Rules

  • The helmet is worn: the bubble holds Kabir's whole head and passes behind Kheelu (clip), so no chalk crosses his face or Kheelu's.
  • Safety shows mechanisms parents can check, then a cta. Never "completely safe".
  • Recognitions are a quiet line of names; logos only with permission.
  • Parents appear only with Tara, and the app shows real-looking data, never selling.
