Tokens

Colour

Two colours from the logo, Chilli (orange) and Kheelu Blue (sky), and Sunshine as the one accent, on cream, white and ink. Brand colours are fills: white on Chilli and Kheelu Blue, ink on Sunshine. On light grounds, text and lines are ink. Chilli is for buying, the logo and brand artwork. Kheelu's rainbow stays on the product. Day is the default; Bedtime is the app's night mode.

Spacing

A 4px base. Sections breathe (space-8/9); cards are cosy (space-5). Components use these steps only; 1–2px nudges that line up an icon or a hairline are the one exception.

Shadow

Flat by default. One tactile ink edge for buttons, one lift for floating things, one soft shadow for stickers.

TokenValueUse

The chunky toy-like base edge under every button (cta, primary, secondary) and the OfferCard: ink in Day, a dark edge in Bedtime. Buttons drop 4px onto it when pressed.

Things arriving: sheets, toasts, stickers popping on. Pair with ease-bounce.
