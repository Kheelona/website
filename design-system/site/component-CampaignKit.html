CampaignKit

One launch campaign: a four-post grid, a WhatsApp share, an email and a Story cover

Open on its own ↗

One launch campaign across channels: a four-post Instagram grid, a WhatsApp share with its message, a deck statement slide, an email and a Story cover. A showcase page, not a bundle export.

Use it for: planning a launch or offer campaign, and briefing anyone who makes posts, emails or ads.

Props: none. Open the preview to copy its markup.

Sections: SocialPost ×4 (Sunshine, Kheelu Blue, Chilli for the offer, the chalkboard) · the 1200×630 WhatsApp preview and the share message · DeckSlide statement · a 600px email (Logo, kh-photo, SectionHeading, two FeatureCards, one cta) · a 9:16 Story cover with the words on the photo's calm wall.

Assets: ira-sticker.png, kabir-sticker.png, tara-sticker.png, kheelu-front-sticker.png, cloud-blue.svg, star-sun.svg, kheelu-on-fur.jpg, myra-02-monsoon-window, myra-03-bedtime-cuddle.

Rules

  • Grid grounds rotate Sunshine, Kheelu Blue, cream, Chilli, chalkboard; Chilli grounds are for offers.
  • Every post keeps the footer pill; one chilli pill per post at most.
  • Stories keep words and the offer in the safe zone (below the top 14%, above the bottom third) and off faces and Kheelu; emails are live text with one cta.
