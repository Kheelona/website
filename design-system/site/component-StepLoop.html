StepLoop

Open on its own ↗

Three numbered steps with handwritten numerals and drawn arrows: the "how it works" loop.

Use it for: how Kheelu works (Talk and play → Kheelu remembers → Knowledge that sticks), setup (unbox → connect → say hello), the reservation process (reserve ₹499 → we ship → pay ₹4,500).

Props: steps (an array of { title, body, tone? }). Tones default to sun, blue, chilli.

Rules

  • Use 3 steps (4 at most). Titles are 2–4 words and bodies one sentence.
  • Steps describe what really happens, in order. No marketing steps ("Step 3: Be amazed").
  • Arrows show only on wide screens. On phones the steps stack.
