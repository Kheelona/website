TrustList

Open on its own ↗

A two-by-two grid of cream panels, each with an icon in a Kheelu Blue circle, a short promise title and one line explaining how it works.

Use it for: "The part that matters most to you": the privacy and safety promises.

Props: items (an array of { icon, title, body }).

Rules

  • Titles are the site's promises: "We do not sell data.", "Kheelu thinks on the device.", "You hold the keys.", "No open internet."
  • Bodies explain the mechanism in one checkable sentence. Link to the Safety page under the grid.
  • Keep the icons in Kheelu Blue: blue is the colour of calm, safety and the parent app.
