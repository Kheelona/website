**Pitch A: the safety angle, to a parenting or tech editor.** Subject: An AI-toy founder who agrees with
the warnings

Hello [name],

You may have covered the Common Sense Media findings on AI toys (January 2026) or Fairplay's advisory
before that. I run Kheelona, a Bengaluru startup building an AI toy for children aged three and up, and
I mostly agree with them. The warnings are a list of design decisions: open internet, no parent in the
loop, engagement without end, a toy that pretends to be alive. Each is a choice a company makes.

We built the other way, and we publish the mechanisms rather than badges: a microphone that sleeps
until a wake word, a closed library instead of the open internet, every conversation readable and
deletable by the parent, no advertising, nothing sold. India's DPDP Act already asks for most of this
for children's data. If a piece on how a parent should judge any AI toy would be useful to your
readers, I am glad to talk, on the record, including about what we have not finished yet (toy-safety
certification is in progress and will be published before we ship).

Our checklist for parents is at kheelona.com/stories/what-to-look-for-in-a-safe-ai-toy.

Apoorva Sahu, co-founder and CEO, Kheelona (Bengaluru)

## 5. Community answers (only where the question is real, with the affiliation stated first)

The trust rule: never post as a neutral parent. Every community reply opens with the affiliation, answers
the actual question, and links only if the thread asks for a resource. A draft for a thread asking
"which screen-free toys actually keep a 3-year-old busy":

"Disclosure first: I work at Kheelona, which makes a talking toy, so read this knowing that. The thing
that has held up in our house is anything that answers back: a grown-up, a grandparent on a call, a
sibling. Toys that only repeat or play phrases last about a week. If you are looking at AI toys, the
two questions I would ask any of them, ours included, are whether it can reach the open internet and
whether you can read everything it said. Happy to share the longer checklist if useful."
