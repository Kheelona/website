## A. Technical SEO

### A1. Three legacy Wix URLs still 404 in Google's index
- **What:** Redirect three old-site paths that Search Console lists as 404s to the closest live page.
- **Where:** `next.config.ts`, inside `redirects()`, in the "Legacy Wix-site URLs" block.
- **The change:**
  ```ts
  { source: "/post/:slug*", destination: "/stories", permanent: true },
  { source: "/terms-conditions", destination: "/terms", permanent: true },
  { source: "/for-the-parents", destination: "/stories", permanent: true },
  ```
- **Why it matters:** Each 404 Google keeps re-crawling is crawl budget not spent on the ten pages it has never fetched, and any link equity on the old URLs is lost.
- **How to verify:** `curl -sI https://kheelona.com/post/anything | grep -i 'HTTP/\|location'` → `308` and `location: /stories`. Same for the other two. `npm test` (`test/redirects-vs-assets.test.ts` passes: no `public/post` or `public/for-the-parents` exists).
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** verified on production 2026-09-05 (commit 4)

### A2. Articles carry real publication and update dates
- **What:** Give every journal article a `published` and `updated` date, taken from git history (never invented), and emit them visibly and in schema.
- **Where:** `src/lib/stories.ts` (type + 4 articles), `src/lib/stories-expansion.ts` (15 articles), `src/app/(site)/stories/[slug]/page.tsx`, `src/app/(site)/stories/page.tsx`, `src/app/sitemap.ts`, `src/config/site.ts` (retire `JOURNAL_REVIEWED`).
- **The change:** `Story` gains `published: string; updated: string` (ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`). BlogPosting gets `datePublished: story.published, dateModified: story.updated`; the byline reads "Published 28 July 2026 · Updated 5 September 2026"; the Blog `blogPost[]` entries carry both; `sitemap.ts` adds `lastModified: s.updated` to story URLs only.
- **Why it matters:** Answer engines weight recency and drop undated content; today all 19 articles claim one hardcoded modification date and no publication date at all.
- **How to verify:** `curl -s https://kheelona.com/stories/should-kids-use-ai | grep -o '"datePublished":"[^"]*","dateModified":"[^"]*"'`; `curl -s https://kheelona.com/sitemap.xml | grep -c lastmod` → 19.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** verified on production 2026-09-05 (commit 2)

### A3. Read time is computed, not typed
- **What:** Replace the hand-set `minutes` on each article with a value derived from its word count.
- **Where:** `src/lib/stories.ts` (`readingMinutes()`), consumers: article byline, `ReadNext`, `/stories` cards, BlogPosting `timeRequired`.
- **The change:** `export const readingMinutes = (s: Story) => Math.max(1, Math.round(words(s) / 200))`.
- **Why it matters:** Articles of 200 to 430 words currently advertise 4 to 6 minute reads, and the schema repeats the inflated number. A page that misstates itself is a page an engine trusts less.
- **How to verify:** byline on a 290-word piece says "2 minute read"; `"timeRequired":"PT2M"` in its JSON-LD.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** verified on production 2026-09-05 (commit 2)

### A4. Articles are `og:type: article`
- **What:** Journal pages declare themselves articles to social and AI crawlers, with published/modified times and author.
- **Where:** `src/lib/seo.ts` `pageMeta()` gains an optional `article` argument; `src/app/(site)/stories/[slug]/page.tsx` passes it.
- **The change:** when `article` is given, `openGraph.type = "article"`, `publishedTime`, `modifiedTime`, `authors`. Default stays `website`.
- **Why it matters:** Sharing and citation tools treat an article differently from a product page; today every URL claims to be a "website".
- **How to verify:** `curl -s <article> | grep -o '<meta property="og:type" content="[^"]*"\|<meta property="article:[^"]*" content="[^"]*"'`.
- **Priority:** P1 · **Status:** verified on production 2026-09-05 (commit 2)

### A5. Breadcrumb schema on the four policy pages
