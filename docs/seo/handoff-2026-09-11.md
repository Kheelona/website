## What matters, before any detail

1. **The AI answer about this company is wrong in five ways, and the site is not the cause.** Seven
   of the ten sources Perplexity cites are stale third-party profiles. Items A1, A2 and A3 are the
   on-site half; the off-site half is section 1 of the founder-actions file and is worth more than
   all of the code here.
2. **Indexing is solved and was never the problem.** 37 pages indexed against a 31-URL sitemap.
   16 of 19 journal articles are indexed and earn zero impressions. Adding pages to a site whose
   pages nobody finds is not the fix; being the answer to a question someone is asking is. That is
   item B1.
3. **Nobody in India searches Google for this category.** Every "AI toy" phrasing returns no volume.
   The demand is in answer engines. This is why AEO and GEO lead and SEO follows, and it is now
   measured rather than asserted.

## A. Entity: make the site the authority on its own facts

### A1. The Product says it used to be called Lumi
- **What:** Tell machines that Kheelu and Lumi are one product, not two.
- **Where:** `src/lib/seo.ts`, `KHEELU_PRODUCT`.
- **The change:** `alternateName: "Lumi"`, beside the `@id` that already reads `lumi`.
- **Why it matters:** Six days after the rename, Perplexity still called the product Lumi, because
  every third-party profile does. This is the field built for exactly that reconciliation.
- **How to verify:** `curl -s https://kheelona.com/products/kheelu | grep -o '"alternateName":"Lumi"'`
  returns one match. Then paste the URL into Google's Rich Results Test and confirm the Product still
  validates.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `7a38300`

### A2. The Product carries its specification as structured properties
- **What:** Publish Kheelu's specs as `PropertyValue` nodes, from the same list the visible table uses.
- **Where:** `src/lib/product-facts.ts` (new, the one source), read by `src/lib/seo.ts` and
  `src/components/molecules/SpecTable.tsx`.
- **The change:** `additionalProperty: productProperties()` and `isFamilyFriendly: true`.
- **Why it matters:** Every competitor snippet Perplexity lifted was spec-dense: a price, an age, a
  language count, in one line. Warm prose gives a retrieval model nothing to put in a comparison row.
- **How to verify:** `curl -s https://kheelona.com/products/kheelu | grep -c '"@type":"PropertyValue"'`
  returns 14. `npx vitest run src/lib/seo.test.ts` passes, including the test that fails if a battery,
  warranty or manufacturing-origin claim ever appears.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `7a38300`

### A3. `llms.txt` lists the facts that are wrong elsewhere
- **What:** A section stating the six facts third-party sources get wrong, and that this site is the
  authority on them.
- **Where:** `src/app/llms.txt/route.ts`, new section above "What Kheelu is"; `LLMS_UPDATED` bumped.
- **The change:** Six bullets: the name (and that Lumi and Kheelu are the same product), plush not
  robot, ages 3+, selling not in beta, three co-founders, WhatsApp-only support.
- **Why it matters:** `llms.txt` is fetched whole by the crawlers that read it. A correction stated
  once, plainly, in a file built for machines is the cheapest way to contest a stale fact.
- **How to verify:** `curl -s https://kheelona.com/llms.txt | grep -A8 "commonly wrong"` shows six
  bullets and the line says "all six of these".
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `2a7a96d`, corrected `0eb985a`

### A4. "Is Kheelu the same as Lumi?" in visible copy
- **What:** Answer the rename as a question on the product page.
- **Where:** `src/app/(site)/products/kheelu/page.tsx`, `FAQ_ITEMS`.
- **The change:** One FAQ entry, which also enters the `FAQPage` schema.
- **Why it matters:** A parent who met this product as Lumi needs to know they are in the right
  place. `FAQPage` may only ever describe visible copy, so putting it in the FAQ is what makes it
  quotable by an answer engine as well as readable by a person.
- **How to verify:** `npm run qa:text -- https://kheelona.com/products/kheelu | grep -i "same as Lumi"`.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `2a7a96d`

## B. Be the answer to the category question

### B1. A buyer's guide at `/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india`
- **What:** A new marketing route answering "how do I choose an AI toy for a small child in India".
- **Where:** `src/app/(site)/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india/page.tsx`. Composed from the registry only:
  `PageHero`, `AnswerBlock`, `StepList`, `SpecTable`, `CompareTable`, `Faq`, `FinaleCTA`.
- **The change:** Five numbered checks, Kheelu's full spec table, the category comparison, a section
  naming what Kheelu has not earned, and eight FAQs. `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` schema. Added to
  the sitemap at priority 0.8, to `FOOTER_LINKS`, and to the `qa:sweep` route list.
- **Why it matters:** Four of the eleven citations in Perplexity's answer to this question are
  vendors' own product pages, so a brand page is eligible to be the answer. Nothing here was shaped
  like one.
- **How to verify:** the URL returns 200; `curl -s .../ai-toys-for-kids-in-india | grep -c '"@type":"Question"'`
  returns 9 (7 FAQs + 2 answer blocks); `SWEEP_STORE=http://store.localhost:3456 npm run qa:sweep`
  reports clean.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `2a7a96d`
- **Deliberately not done:** no competitor is named and no competitor's specification appears. This
  site cannot verify another company's child-safety behaviour, and publishing a wrong number about
  how a rival's toy handles a child's voice is the failure the brand veto exists to prevent. If
  verified rival specs ever arrive from the founder, this is the page they belong on.

### B2. `SpecTable`, and the three specs that are deliberately absent
- **What:** A molecule rendering `KHEELU_FACTS` as a real `<table>` with row headers.
- **Where:** `src/components/molecules/SpecTable.tsx` (+ test, + story).
- **The change:** 17 rows, of which 3 answer "not announced yet" in muted ink and never reach schema.
- **Why it matters:** Battery life, warranty terms and country of manufacture are what a rival table
  carries and this product has not published. Stating that plainly is more useful to a parent than a
  guess, and it makes it impossible for a model to hallucinate a number into the gap.
- **How to verify:** `npx vitest run src/components/molecules/SpecTable.test.tsx` — 8 tests, including
  one that fails if a number ever appears in a pending row and one that fails on any
  "made in / built in" string.
- **Priority:** P0 · **Status:** shipped `7a38300`

## D. A latent bug the gate caught, recorded because it will recur

- **What:** `CompareTable` greys its "No" cells with `text-ink-muted`, which is 4.21:1 on the cool
  wash and 4.20:1 on sun. Both are under WCAG AA and both were **already banned** in
  `test/contrast-tokens.test.ts`.
- **Why it survived:** the component had been written and then **never rendered on any route**, so no
  sweep had ever seen it. Its first placement, on B1, put it on a cool room, and `qa:sweep` failed
  four nodes at 1280px within a minute.
- **The fix:** the room moved to cream, where the same ink is 4.52:1. The constraint is now written
  on the component itself.
- **The general law (§8.36-c):** a component that is not rendered on any route is not covered by any
  gate, however many tests it has. The sweep checks pages, not exports.
- **Worth noting:** on the same page the sweep correctly waved through nine white-on-orange §8.29
  pairs while catching these four. The narrow accept-matcher is doing exactly what it was built for.
- **Status:** fixed `2a7a96d`

## Not doing, and why

- **No `aggregateRating` or `review` schema.** There are no real reviews. Verified there is none in
  the codebase today.
- **No named-competitor comparison table.** Unverifiable rival specs; brand-trust veto. See B1.
- **No head-term keyword campaign.** 44 queries in three months, DR 0. The terms with volume are
  served by C1 as a long bet, honestly labelled.
- **No redirect for `checkout.kheelona.com`.** It has no DNS record at all, so it does not resolve
  and nothing in this repo can reach it. It ages out of the index by itself.
- **No fix for the nine 404s in Search Console.** Eight of nine already redirect correctly, verified
  with curl; they were last crawled before the 2026-09-05 redirects shipped and are stale report
  entries. The ninth is `checkout.` above.
- **No bulk "request indexing".** 16 of the URLs anyone would submit are already indexed. Only
  `/products/kheelu` and the four new URLs are worth a click.
