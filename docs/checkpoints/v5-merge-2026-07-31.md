## Why the method matters more than any single fix

The Chrome extension could not do this review: its window is locked at ~390px and its tab runs
hidden, which freezes IntersectionObserver reveals and defers image painting. Screenshots from it
would have shown half-empty pages and called them layout. So the round ran through a headless
harness — exact viewports, reveals forced, lazy images warmed, every `<section>` clipped, plus a
numeric audit of 9 routes at two viewports. **Every finding below is a number, not an impression**,
and that is the reusable part. §8.23 records the harness so it can be rebuilt.

## Found by the review, reported by nobody

1. **Kheelu appeared twice in the hero viewport.** REV-a's final art contains him and the corner
   guide rendered him simultaneously. The guide now waits out any hero marked
   `[data-hero-has-kheelu]`. Scope is deliberate: the 46px mobile dock avatar is UI chrome and keeps
   its Reserve CTA.
2. **Home and /products/lumi were 60% the same page** — six shared components. The duplicate
   feelings gallery came off the product page (17.0k → 15.8k px on mobile).
3. **A real contrast failure in `FootnotesRow`** (13px `text-ink-muted` ≈ 4.3:1 on the sun wash)
   that three prior "axe zero violations" sweeps were structurally unable to see, because axe skips
   `opacity-0` subtrees and nobody had forced reveals first. This is the most important process
   lesson of the round (§8.23-6).
4. Hero composition: art was 420px in an 800px hero with empty wash above it → 470/500px desktop.
5. The line "Your guide is waiting in the corner" was removed — the guide fade made it untrue.
