## Why the round exists

Parents who saw the live site said: *"it's good that you are teaching, but what will a kid who
buys this at 2 GET when they are 5?"* — and the site had no answer anywhere. The hero
("Your kid's favourite tutor. Their best friend first.") was called very confusing. The founder
ran the round through their own four-step process: a Joanna Wiebe conversion-copy persona drafted
(Step 2), an independent senior content QA reviewed with fresh context (Step 3), and only its
APPROVE releases the publish (Step 4). Positioning mix unchanged: 40% fun / 20% brain development
/ 40% education; Lumi 2 to 5, platform 2 to 14.

## Post-approval copy tweak (founder, 2026-07-31, after the QA verdict)

The growth-room kickers spell the unit out: **"At 2 years" / "At 3 years" / "At 4 years" /
"By 5 years"** (was "At 2" … "By 5"). Founder instruction, and the right call — a standalone
uppercase label has no sentence around it to say *2 of what*, unlike the prose uses
("A best friend at 2", "First words at 2 become…"), which are deliberately left alone along with
the approved hero. One data edit in `lib/growth-arc.ts`, its `LUMI_AGES` guard test updated to
match, and the wording propagated to every doc that quotes the kickers. `QA-V6-note.md` is
deliberately NOT edited: it records what the reviewer saw at review time, and rewriting a review
record to match later copy would falsify it. Verified after the change: 276 tests, tsc clean, build
green, kickers present in the served HTML with the old form gone, and both viewports re-shot — the
longer labels hold one line at 390px and 1440px.
