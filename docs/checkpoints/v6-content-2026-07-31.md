## Why the round exists

Parents who saw the live site said: *"it's good that you are teaching, but what will a kid who
buys this at 2 GET when they are 5?"* — and the site had no answer anywhere. The hero
("Your kid's favourite tutor. Their best friend first.") was called very confusing. The founder
ran the round through their own four-step process: a Joanna Wiebe conversion-copy persona drafted
(Step 2), an independent senior content QA reviewed with fresh context (Step 3), and only its
APPROVE releases the publish (Step 4). Positioning mix unchanged: 40% fun / 20% brain development
/ 40% education; Lumi 2 to 5, platform 2 to 14.

## The founder's four decisions (one question batch)

Claims sourced from published facts only · hero = the outcome arc, tutor narrative relocated (my
call where) · **AI mode needs home WiFi, Kheelu-mode stories and Bluetooth music work offline**
(the flat "No, Lumi plays offline" FAQ answer was WRONG on the live site) · soft school-readiness
frame licensed, no guarantees. Plus at spec review: the growth room's Kheelu line ("From first
words to big ideas. I'm there."), the Home meta title, and en-IN spelling.

## Found by the round, reported by nobody

1. **The settled-axe discipline** (§8.24-5e): axe fired immediately after forcing reveals is
   unstable — with a ~1.5s settle it surfaced **four real, pre-existing contrast failures on
   /products/lumi** (live on kheelona.com today) that every earlier "axe zero" sweep missed.
   Fixed: blue-ink is the numeral blue, muted text left the tinted washes. Re-run: 0 violations.
2. **The independent QA earned its independence** — it REJECTED the first pass with four real
   blockers everyone else missed, the worst being that **/privacy described the retired six-field
   Tally form** (email + birth month are no longer collected, and the published leave-the-list
   route "reply to any email" could not work). Also: /terms' "everyone" vs the 500 cap, one flat
   "ten languages" in the journal, four em-dashes on /pricing.md (machine routes were outside the
   voice-lint sweep — they are in it now). All fixed, re-verified line by line, verdict APPROVE.
3. Harness facts for §8.23 practitioners: lazy images need dwell; await only visible images; the
   Tally iframe never paints beyond-viewport (judge it from an in-view clip); >16384px pages need
   a reduced deviceScaleFactor.
