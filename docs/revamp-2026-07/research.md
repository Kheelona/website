## Executive synthesis (what the copy must do)

1. **The category's fear script was written for Lumi to answer.** The Nov 2025 – Feb 2026
   AI-toy investigation wave (PIRG Trouble in Toyland, Fairplay advisory ~80 orgs, Common
   Sense Media risk assessment, FoloToy Kumma explicit-content scandal, Bondu transcript
   exposure, Miko senate letters, Moxie's cloud-death) means every parent search about AI toys
   is now a safety search. Lumi's actual spec — full conversation log, closed library, wake-word
   mic, on-device first — matches the watchdog checklist almost line for line. Copy shows the
   MECHANISM, never just a badge (Miko wore kidSAFE+COPPA seals two months before its data
   exposure went public).
2. **Category language shifted**: say "screen-free AI toy" / "AI plush companion" / "talking AI
   toy", demote "AI robot toy" (robot = Miko/screens/wheels intent — the thing Lumi is not).
3. **The India incumbent to beat is Miko** (₹17,990–19,490 + ₹2,999/yr Max subscription; screen;
   Feb 2026 exposure story; "unusable for a 3 year old" reviews). Copy never names it: "No
   screen. No subscription surprise. Read every word." does the work. (Subscription claim is
   FOUNDER-GATED — see open facts.)
4. **Direct price-lane rivals** (new since the old keyword map): MyWonder (Bengaluru pod+cards,
   ₹4,999–5,399, ages 3–10, "screen-free conversational AI", FirstCry + Bengaluru retail);
   IFITech Buddy (AI plush, ₹9,990, 8 Indian languages, parent chat log — closest spec overlap,
   zero reviews); Cheeko (Alto AI, cards, ages 3–6, unpriced). Differentiate on companion +
   10 home languages + verifiable parental visibility, NOT on price or "screen-free" alone.
5. **Languages is an open lane**: nobody owns "AI toy that speaks Hindi/Tamil/Kannada"
   authoritatively. Pair with the grandparent / joint-family scene (NavParent: grandparents
   handing the phone is the most common Indian screen pattern). "Your child answers your
   Kannada in English" is the emotional nerve.
6. **Novelty decay is the unspoken objection** (Curio Grok "sat on the shelf after a week").
   Sell daily rituals (bedtime story in Nani's language, morning why-questions), not "wow, it
   talks".
7. **On-device-first is three claims in one**: privacy + no-WiFi-tantrums + longevity ("what if
   you shut down?" — the Moxie question; consider the FAQ entry).
8. **India-native trust language**: DPDP Rules notified 13 Nov 2025 — verifiable parental
   consent for under-18s, ban on tracking/profiling/targeted ads at children. Imports talk
   COPPA. Claim alignment ONLY per counsel (existing privacy/terms gate).

### Tier-2 India entrants (the real shelf-mates)

- **MyWonder** (Bengaluru, Antler-backed, $300K pre-seed, Google for Startups): screen-free AI
  pod + physical cards, launch ₹4,999 w/ 10 cards, now ₹5,399 (MRP ₹8,999), cards ₹399–999,
  ages 3–10, "No internet browsing or third-party ads are ever played", FirstCry + Bengaluru
  try-before-buy. **Same price point, same age band as Lumi.**
- **IFITech Buddy**: AI plush bunny ₹9,990 (from ₹14,999), Hindi/English/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali/
  Gujarati/Marathi/Urdu, parent chat history + summaries. Closest spec overlap; zero reviews;
  needs 2.4GHz Wi-Fi; no stated library curation.
- **Cheeko** (Alto AI): device + RFID cards, ages 3–6, En/Hi/Ta/Te/Kn/Ml, unpriced (Toy Biz
  Expo 2026, New Delhi).
- **Bondu** (US, $199.99): $5.3M seed; then the Feb 2026 50,000-transcript exposure (Axios).
- **Moxie** (Embodied, $800): DEAD Dec 2024 — cloud-dependent robots bricked, children grieved.
  The "what if you shut down" question is now standard.
- **FoloToy Kumma** ($99 GPT-4o teddy): PIRG caught explicit sexual content + knife/match
  advice; pulled, OpenAI cut access; the incident that colours every AI-toy search since.

### Safety-messaging patterns

- **Badges don't inoculate** (Miko: kidSAFE+COPPA PR in Dec 2025, senate letter in Feb 2026).
  Show mechanisms: transcript, on-device, closed library, one-tap delete.
- **Absence claims work** (Yoto: "no microphones or cameras") — Lumi's version: "no open
  internet, no ads, and you can read every word."
- **Visibility is the feature** (Curio's transcripts + parent prompt were its best-reviewed
  safety feature; PIRG's checklist: time limits + FULL transcripts + parental controls, and
  "none of the toys tested included all"). Lumi's spec matches the wishlist — rare case where
  the safest claim is the differentiator.
- **Curation claims** (MyWonder) are the closest Indian analogue to the closed-library message;
  expect side-by-side comparison.

### Parent voice (sourced phrases; Reddit was unfetchable — journalists/experts/testers)

- Fear of outputs: Kumma "would take a single sexual topic… and run with it" (PIRG via CNN);
  toys that "offer advice on where a child can find matches or knives" (PIRG).
- Fear of attachment: children "are wired to attach… incredibly risky to give them an AI toy
  that they will see as sentient" (Dr. Jenny Radesky, Fairplay advisory); Common Sense Media:
  no AI toys under 5, extreme caution 6–12. **The /safety copy must answer this head-on,
  specifically, without hand-waving — highest-stakes block on the site.**
- Fear of surveillance: "kids confide in their favorite toys" → toymakers hold "deeply personal
  thoughts, emotions, fears" (Yahoo); precedents: Hello Barbie 2015, CloudPets.
- Incumbent buyer's remorse: "Do not buy", "Waste of money" (Miko Trustpilot snippets); without
  the subscription "Miko simply isn't as good".
- India screen-time anguish: LocalCircles (57k+ parents): 49% of 9–17s at 3+ hrs/day; 66% "my
  child is addicted"; 58% of Indian mothers "angry mom" during screen conflicts (2022, verify
  before quoting); grandparents-handing-the-phone = the joint-family scene.

### Pricing context

₹4,999 pre-order = MyWonder's exact lane (they include 10 cards); ₹9,999 post-launch sits on
IFITech Buddy's ₹9,990 and well under Miko Mini ₹13,999. The category trains parents to see
slashed-MRP framing (Buddy ₹14,999→9,990; MyWonder ₹8,999→5,399), so "₹9,999 after launch" is
credible. Pre-order lessons: lead with the safety story in the pre-order itself (Bondu);
concrete dates + touchable demos convert (Toniebox 2, MyWonder retail trials) — never invent a
date; don't ship a beta (Curio's 2023 interrupting-toy reviews still rank in 2026);
subscription-forward pricing breeds resentment (Miko's ₹37,999 bundle).
