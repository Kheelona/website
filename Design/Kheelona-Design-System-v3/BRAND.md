## 1. Brand Overview

**Kheelona in one sentence.** Safe, conversational AI tutors for kids aged 2–5 — AI-powered physical toys that blend robotics, great content, and play, wrapped in a warm Pixar-feeling world of orange and sky blue.

### 1.1 Purpose
> To make learning, curiosity, and meaningful conversation a natural part of childhood.

### 1.2 Vision
> To build sharper minds and bigger smiles for kids everywhere.

### 1.3 Mission
> We build safe, conversational AI tutors for kids, combining robotics, great content, and play.

### 1.4 Positioning Statement
> For parents seeking meaningful early-childhood learning, Kheelona is the interactive play ecosystem that delivers cognitive development at scale by blending AI-powered physical toys with engaging, age-appropriate play experiences.

### 1.5 Target Audiences
1. **Parents of kids aged 2–5** — the primary buyer and trust gatekeeper.
2. **Tier-1 cities, Bangalore first** — geographic beachhead.
3. **Top-tier play schools** — institutional channel.

## 2. Tone of Voice

**The summary:** *We sound like a smart, warm and slightly playful friend — one who understands children, respects parents, and makes advanced technology feel simple, magical and trustworthy.*

**The cheat-sheet:** Smart, not academic · Playful, not childish · Warm, not sentimental · Premium, not pretentious · Futuristic, not technical · Protective, not fearful · Character-led, not gimmicky · Confident, not corporate.

### 2.1 The eight voice principles

| # | Principle | How we sound | We avoid |
|---|-----------|--------------|----------|
| 1 | **Playful, Not Childish** | We are witty, curious and fun. We make children smile without talking down to them. | Baby talk, excessive cuteness, forced jokes, or overly childish language. |
| 2 | **Warm & Empathetic** | We speak like a caring companion who understands children and parents. Our language feels reassuring, human and emotionally aware. | Cold, clinical, corporate or overly sentimental communication. |
| 3 | **Simple & Accessible** | We explain sophisticated technology in words anyone can understand. Short, clear sentences. One idea at a time. | Jargon, technical complexity, long explanations or unnecessarily clever language. |
| 4 | **Intelligent, Not Academic** | We communicate the intelligence behind our product with confidence, while keeping the experience approachable and enjoyable. | Academic lectures, educational superiority, or making parents feel uninformed. |
| 5 | **Character-Led & Story-Driven** | Our characters have opinions, emotions and personalities. We let people experience the product through stories and interactions. | Treating the character as merely a mascot or constantly talking about product features. |
| 6 | **Modern & Imaginative** | We talk about the future with optimism and wonder. Technology feels magical, useful and human — not intimidating. | Cold futuristic jargon, exaggerated sci-fi claims or technology for technology's sake. |
| 7 | **Protective & Trustworthy** | When discussing children, safety or parents, we become clear, responsible and reassuring. Parents should feel they can trust us. | Fear-based messaging, manipulative parenting language, alarmism or making unsupported promises. |
| 8 | **Premium & Confident** | We are confident in what we build. Our words are thoughtful, purposeful and refined, while still feeling approachable. | Loud self-praise, excessive superlatives, discount-brand language or sounding overly corporate. |

## 4. Logo (summary — full spec in `guidelines/logo.md`)

The wordmark is a rounded, sticker-style "Kheelona" in Deep Orange and Sky Blue with a white outline.

- **The 'K' (Heart) — Care & Companionship.** Grounds the brand in emotional warmth. It signals that companionship and a safe environment are the foundation of the Kheelona experience.
- **The 'O' (4-Point Star) — Playful Tech Ed.** Represents the dynamic energy of modern learning. It turns the letterform into a symbol of future-focused, engaging technology education.

## 5. Color (summary — full spec in `guidelines/color.md`)

> Kheelona's palette is warm-first and tech-second. Dominant Yellow and White (60%) evoke optimism and screen-free clarity, anchored by Sky Blue (20%) for reliable intelligence. Deep Orange adds a burst of energy, while subtle Teal and Purple accents (10%) provide the "magical spark" of AI discovery. Together, they position Kheelona as a sophisticated learning companion rather than a simple plastic toy.

| Color | Hex | Role | Usage |
|-------|-----|------|:-----:|
| Golden Yellow | `#F1A23B` | Optimism, happiness | 30% |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Screen-free clarity | 30% |
| Sky Blue | `#29A0D7` | Trust, logic, technology anchor | 20% |
| Deep Orange | `#EF762F` | Energy, physical activity | 10% |
| Teal | `#1ABC9C` | Cognitive Freshness — the *result* of the tech | 5% |
| Purple | `#8B5BFF` | Magic Spark — mystery, premium | 5% |

## 6. Typography (summary — full spec in `guidelines/typography.md`)

- **Primary — Glory.** Geometric sans-serif with distinctively rounded, soft curves. Friendly and bouncy without looking like a childish comic font; aligns with the Pixar/Disney aesthetic. Weights: Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold.
- **Secondary — Instrument Sans.** A "precision" neo-grotesque: sharp, modern, highly organized — legible with a "smart" feel. Weights: Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold.
- **Editorial accent — Instrument Serif (Italic).** Used for editorial section titles in brand collateral.

**Heading style:** Glory · letter-spacing −1% · line-height 120%.
**Paragraph style:** Instrument Sans · letter-spacing +1% · line-height 130% · reference sizes 36 (subhead) / 24 (body).

## 7. Mascot (summary — full spec in `guidelines/mascot.md`)

The mascot is a **Foxy-Deer** — the body and tail of a fox with the antlers of a deer.

- **Fox** → intelligence, curiosity, cleverness, playfulness.
- **Deer** → gentleness, grace, connection to nature.
- **Combined** → smart *and* gentle: toys that are cleverly designed but safe and approachable, with a magical, creative approach to play.
- **Glasses** → visual shorthand for knowledge, learning, studiousness, creativity.
- **Cargo shorts & patches (acorn, leaf)** → nature, hiking, scouting: adventure, discovery, learning about the world through play.

The mascot wears an orange tee with the white Kheelona K-mark on the chest.

## 8. Decorative System

Seven rounded geometric shapes (`assets/shapes/`) — flowers, squircle, polygon, triangle — used as playful background decorators, plus noise textures (`assets/textures/`) for tactile surfaces. Recolor shapes with brand colors via `currentColor`; keep them subordinate to content.
