# Mascot Guidelines

**Kheelona Brand Guidelines v3.0 · pages 25–27**

## 1. Character Design (p.25)

The Kheelona mascot is a **Foxy-Deer** — a hybrid creature with the body and tail of a fox and the antlers of a deer.

> The Fox aspect usually represents intelligence, curiosity, cleverness, and playfulness.
> The Deer aspect represents gentleness, grace, and a connection to nature.
> Combined: it suggests a mascot that is both smart and gentle — toys that are cleverly designed but safe and approachable for children. It also points toward a magical or creative approach to play.

**Outfit & accessories — Learning and Exploration:**
> The Glasses: in character design these are a visual shorthand for knowledge, learning, studiousness, and creativity.
> The Cargo Shorts and Patches (Acorn and Leaf): these directly reference nature, hiking, and scouting. They signify a brand focused on adventure, discovery, and learning about the world through play.

Signature elements that must stay consistent:
- Round **Sky Blue glasses** (`#29A0D7` frames).
- **Deep Orange tee** with the **white K-mark** on the chest.
- **Beige cargo shorts** with acorn/leaf patches; brown sneakers.
- Fluffy fox tail, cream-tipped; small cream deer antlers; rosy cheeks.

## 3. Usage Rules

1. The mascot is a **character, not a sticker** — give it something to do (point, read, celebrate) related to the content. Voice principle 5: character-led, never "merely a mascot".
2. Never redraw, recolor, or dress the mascot differently; new poses must match `reference/`.
3. Never flip renders that contain the chest K-mark (it would mirror the mark).
4. Keep the mascot large enough that the face reads (≥ 96 px tall on screen).
5. One mascot instance per composition unless showing a story sequence.
6. Leave the mascot's face unobstructed by text or badges.
