Why it matters

Conversation helps a young brain grow.

Harvard’s Center on the Developing Child says back-and-forth “serve and return” exchanges between a young child and a caring adult help build and strengthen neural connections in the child’s brain. They also support the early language and social skills that later, more complex thinking builds on.[1]

What is Kheelu?

Kheelu by Kheelona is a screen-free plush rabbit that talks with children aged 3 to 7. It answers your child, then asks a question back. It has three modes: AI mode for open conversation on home Wi‑Fi, Story mode for stories, poems and jingles with no Wi‑Fi needed, and Bluetooth mode, which makes it a speaker for your phone.

Safety

Safety you can check.

You’ll always see when it’s listening.

Its microphone turns on only when someone presses the wake-up button. It stays on while your child talks and switches itself off after about a minute of quiet. A light on Kheelu shows whenever it’s listening. Left alone, it hears nothing and sends nothing.

How it understands and answers.

To understand and answer your child, Kheelu uses trusted speech and AI providers, on servers in India. They process your child’s voice only to produce Kheelu’s reply. They don’t keep it, and they never use it to train their AI. Our contracts with them say so.

Questions

Questions parents ask.

Won’t an AI toy make my child think less?

That’s exactly what Kheelu is designed against. Most AI gives answers. Kheelu answers your child, then asks them a question back, so they do the thinking. It can’t browse the internet, and when it doesn’t know something, it says so instead of guessing. You can read every conversation in the parent app.

What ages is Kheelu for?

Kheelu is made for children aged 3 to 7. It is not a toy for children under 3. For 3-year-olds, we advise having a grown-up nearby. When you set it up, you enter your child’s age in the parent app, and every reply passes an age-graded safety layer set to that age. You can change it later.

How much is Kheelu, and how does the advance work?

Kheelu is ₹4,999 (incl. GST) for the first 500 units, with delivery anywhere in India included. Its MRP is ₹7,999 (incl. of all taxes). A refundable ₹499 advance reserves yours today, and the remaining ₹4,500 is due before delivery. You can pay by UPI, card or net banking, and you get a GST invoice.

What if it isn’t right for us?

Return it within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked. Opened is fine: just send back everything that came in the box, undamaged. We arrange the pickup, free. Once it reaches us and qualifies for return, your refund is processed within 7 working days. Until we dispatch it, everything you’ve paid is refundable in full.

Deliveries start mid-November. Pre-orders are served first, in the order they were placed, and you get a tracking link when it leaves us. Delivery is included anywhere in India, with nothing to pay at the door. We don’t ship outside India. A reserved Kheelu is sent once the rest of the price is paid.
