Safety

Are AI toys safe? Check ours, fact by fact.

Not all of them, so check before you buy one for a child aged 3 to 7. Ask when its microphone is on, how it understands your child, whether it can browse the internet, and whether you can read every conversation. Here’s how Kheelu answers each one, in plain words.

Four facts

Is Kheelu safe for my child?

Judge it by what it does. Here are the four facts that matter most, in full.

  1. {{icon:microphone}}

    Is Kheelu always listening?

    No. Kheelu has no wake word to listen for. Its microphone turns on only when someone presses the wake-up button. It stays on while your child is talking and switches itself off after about a minute of quiet. Until the next press, nothing is heard, recorded or sent. A light on Kheelu shows whenever it’s listening.

  2. {{icon:lock-simple}}

    How does Kheelu understand and answer my child?

    To understand and answer your child, Kheelu uses trusted speech and AI providers, on servers in India. They process your child’s voice only to produce Kheelu’s reply. They don’t keep it, and they never use it to train their AI. Our contracts with them say so.

  3. {{icon:eye}}

    Can I read every conversation?

    Yes, in the parent app, word for word. Delete any of them in one tap.

  4. {{icon:hand-heart}}

    Do you sell my child’s data?

    No. We don’t sell your data, and your child’s data is never used to advertise to them.

Questions

Questions parents ask.

Is Kheelu OK for a 3-year-old?{{icon:plus}}

Kheelu is made for children aged 3 to 7, and it is not a toy for children under 3. If your child is 3, we advise play with a grown-up nearby. Common Sense Media advises against AI toys at this age. Its advice in full, and our answer, follow below.

What do child-safety groups say about AI toys?{{icon:plus}}

In January 2026, Common Sense Media advised parents not to give AI toys, which it describes as “smart toys with voice-based interactions”, to children aged 5 and under, and to use extreme caution before buying them for children aged 6 to 12.[1] We make Kheelu for ages 3 to 7, so you should hear this from us.

When it tested three AI toys, it rated 27% of their replies as not appropriate for kids. It also raised concerns about toys designed to create emotional attachment, the data they collect and how reliably they work.[2]

Here is what Kheelu does about each concern:

  • Replies: its answers come from a curated, closed content library, and every reply passes an age-graded safety layer. When it doesn’t know something, it says so. Before launch, we test it by trying to make it say things it shouldn’t. You can read every conversation.
  • Attachment: Kheelu tells your child it’s a toy, and it never asks them to keep a secret from you.
  • Data: the four facts at the top of this page. We don’t sell your data.
  • How well it works: if it isn’t right for your family, return it within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked.
  • At 3: we advise play with a grown-up nearby.

Sources [1] Common Sense Media, “Common Sense Media Warns Against AI Toy Companions After Research Reveals Safety Risks” (press release), 2026, paragraph 1. commonsensemedia.org

[2] Common Sense Media Youth AI Safety Institute, “AI Toys” (risk assessment), 2026, “What we found”. institute.commonsensemedia.org
