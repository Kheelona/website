Questions before you pay

What am I paying today?

A refundable ₹499 today reserves your Kheelu, and it is part of the ₹4,999 price (incl. GST), not an extra fee. The remaining ₹4,500 is due before delivery, by a payment link we send on WhatsApp and by email. Nothing is ever charged automatically.

What if I change my mind?

Until we dispatch your Kheelu, message us with your order number and you get all of your money back, with no fee and no reason needed. We start your refund within 2 working days of your request; your bank takes 5-7 working days. After delivery, you can still return it within 7 days, no questions asked.

When will my Kheelu arrive?

Deliveries start mid-November. Pre-orders go out first, in the order they were placed, and we send you a tracking link when yours leaves us. Delivery anywhere in India is included, with nothing to pay at the door. We don’t ship outside India. A reserved Kheelu is dispatched once the balance is paid.

How can I pay?

By UPI, debit or credit card, or net banking, in Razorpay’s payment window. There are no payment fees and nothing recurring. Your card number, UPI ID and bank details go to Razorpay, not to us, and every order comes with a GST invoice.
