**How LP stayed off production (the method, stronger than test plan §5.1).**

1. Chromium was launched with `--host-resolver-rules="MAP * 127.0.0.1:8443, EXCLUDE localhost, EXCLUDE *.localhost,
   EXCLUDE 127.0.0.1"`, so the browser itself could resolve no host but loopback.
2. A local TLS proxy on 8443 served a throwaway self-signed certificate (`ignoreHTTPSErrors`). It forwarded only
   `kheelona.com` and `store.kheelona.com` to the local build. Any `/ingest` path that is not a GET of
   `/ingest/array/*` or `/ingest-assets/*`, any `/_vercel` path and any other host were recorded and answered there,
   never forwarded. Redirect hops and page-unload beacons, which leave Playwright's routing (QA-001), therefore stayed
   local too.
3. Playwright routing answered every beacon before it left the page: GA4 collect on `google-analytics.com`,
   `analytics.google.com` and `www.google.com`, all answered 204. Meta `facebook.com/tr` (200 with a 1 × 1 GIF) and
   the gateway `*.on.aws/events` and `*.run.app` (200). PostHog `/ingest/**` events. Ahrefs `/api/*`, and Ahrefs'
   own script (answered empty: it is ungated, and its tag is proven by curl, row 3). Vercel `/_vercel/*`. Every other
   third-party request was recorded and answered 204.
4. The tags' script and config downloads (`www.googletagmanager.com/gtag/js`, `connect.facebook.net/en_US/fbevents.js`,
   `connect.facebook.net/signals/{config,plugins}/…`) were fetched by Node with GET and served from a cache. PostHog's
   `config.js` and SDK files went through the local server's rewrite.

**Result.** Final pages run (16 routes): 0 escaped requests, proxy hosts only `kheelona.com` and `store.kheelona.com`
(`network/lp-probe-controls-control-host-pages-1791945304121.json`). Flows run: 15 escaped PostHog unload POSTs, all
answered by the proxy (`lp-probe-flows-1791945038330.json`). Store run: 2 escaped, the `purchase` spy and a `$$heatmap`
(`lp-probe-store-1791945087959.json`). Event run: 1 escaped `$snapshot`. No request reached a third-party host
outside the GET download list, and nothing was forwarded beyond the local build.
