# QA baseline of the live site (2026-10-10)

The "before" picture of kheelona.com and store.kheelona.com for the reset's before/after comparison
(brief section 6, QA, Phase 1). Measured on **production**, because every measurement tag is
host-gated and a local run scores a page missing its tags
([lessons](../engineering/lessons.md#6-seo-infrastructure)). Captures ran 2026-10-11 02:03-05:17
UTC, which is the evening of 2026-10-10 in US Pacific time. Production was only read: GET requests,
one at a time, no forms, no payments.

## In one screen

- **Mobile Performance misses 90 on three money or landing pages**: Home 86, store 85,
  `/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india` 80 (medians of 3). Product page 90. Mobile LCP medians over 2.5 s on
  Home (2.59 s), `/playos` (2.66 s) and `/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india` (3.24 s). CLS is clean
  everywhere (at most 0.045). Lab TBT is at most 190 ms, but **field INP on Home phones is 546 ms**
  (PostHog p75), so the lab hides the interaction problem.
- **Best Practices is 73 on every page and every form factor.** Root cause: the Meta pixel's `fr`
  third-party cookie costs 19 of the 27 lost points (G10, known); a console error from our own
  Report-Only CSP and an inspector issue (Meta's cookie, plus a CSP report on Meta's gateway) cost
  the other 8.
- **Accessibility is 96 on every page.** The only failure, in both Lighthouse and axe, is white on
  `#EF762F` at 2.87:1, the founder-accepted CTA pair (G2). axe finds **0 critical, 0 other serious,
  0 moderate, 0 minor** issues on all 36 pages at 390 and 1280 px.
- **SEO is 100 on every indexable page**; the store is 66 because it is `noindex` (known). One
  desktop median of 92 is a lab timeout on `robots.txt`, not a site fault.
- **No broken internal links.** All 35 sitemap URLs and the store answer 200 with no redirect; all
  36 internal link targets and all 156 first-party asset and metadata references answer 200 with no
  redirect; no internal link points at a redirecting URL; no missing `#fragment`.
- **Metadata**: one H1, a clean heading order, a self canonical, `lang="en-IN"`, complete Open Graph
  and Twitter tags and alt text (or a deliberate empty alt) on all 35 indexable pages. But **9 of 36
  titles exceed 60 characters and 8 of 35 indexable descriptions exceed 155**, every page shares one
  `og:image`, and **no page shows visible breadcrumbs**.
- **JSON-LD parses on all 35 indexable pages and validates clean** (31 of 36 URLs checked by
  validator.schema.org: 0 errors, 0 warnings). Against brief 9.2 the Product Offer lacks a return policy and shipping
  details, the audience has no upper age, and the spec list still carries the retired microphone
  wording.
- **Phones (360 x 640):** the primary CTA is in the first screen on Home and the product page, and a
  sticky CTA sits at the bottom of every marketing page. But on Home the sticky bar **covers the
  price caption** under the hero CTA (cut at 360 x 640, hidden at 360 x 590), its own CTA is 38 px
  tall, and every page has 16 or more controls under 44 px. No real sideways scroll at 324, 360 or
  412 px.
- **Three findings that are new and are not page defects** (sections 7.1 to 7.3): the store renders
  in a US data centre (`iad1`) for Indian visitors; the CSP does not list the Meta gateway the pixel
  sends to; and Meta's automatic matching attaches **Kheelona's own support number** to visitors as
  their phone.

**Lighthouse pages.** Home, product, store and the top landing pages by sessions
(`/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india`, `/team`; [PostHog audit, landing pages](../research/posthog-audit.md#landing-pages-by-host-q12)),
plus `/safety`, `/stories`, `/playos`, `/privacy` and one article as the template for all 22.

**Medians.** Each score and each metric is the median of its own 3 runs (per-metric median, not
Lighthouse's "median run"). Run-to-run spread is wide (for example `/team` mobile Performance 55, 92,
99), consistent with network noise on the test path: the same machine measured cached responses at
0.85 to 11.7 s to first byte through Vercel's Mumbai edge at 02:57 UTC
([ttfb](../raw/qa/2026-10-10/crawl/ttfb-2026-10-11T0257Z.txt)) and 16 to 150 ms through San
Francisco at 05:00 UTC ([refs crawl](../raw/qa/2026-10-10/crawl/refs-2026-10-11T0457Z.json.gz)).
Treat lab Performance as a direction and field data from PostHog as the measure.

**INP.** A Lighthouse navigation run cannot measure INP; none of the 60 reports has a value. TBT is
reported instead, with the field INP from PostHog beside it.

**Caveat for the analytics baseline.** The Lighthouse runs could not block measurement without
changing the Best Practices result, so they reached the live tools. Loads that requested each
tool: GA4 60, Vercel Analytics 60, Ahrefs 60, PostHog 59, Meta pixel 57, between 2026-10-11 02:03
and 02:46 UTC, user agents "moto g
power (2022) Chrome/154" (mobile) and "Macintosh Chrome/154" (desktop). Every other QA run aborted
those requests. Exclude that window from traffic counts.

### Where the time goes (mobile, a median-scoring run per page)

- **Server response is the largest LCP part**: 1.2 to 1.6 s of time to first byte in Lighthouse's
  LCP breakdown on Home, the product page, `/safety` and the store, against 0.9 to 1.3 s of image
  load. The store's LCP element is a text hint under the form, not an image, so its LCP is server
  response plus render. See 7.1 for the store.
- **Script is half the bytes**: on Home 882 of 1,765 KiB in 25 requests; third parties 433 KiB
  (Meta 253 KiB, Google Tag Manager 177 KiB). Lighthouse estimates 325 KiB of unused JavaScript and
  64 KiB of legacy polyfills; main-thread work 2.9 s at 4x CPU.
- **One image costs most of the image budget**: the animated `first-conversations.webp` on Home is
  307 KiB with 289 KiB estimated savings.
- **LCP element**: the hero plush image on Home, the product page, `/safety` and
  `/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india` (412 px emulation); the Magic Box photo on `/playos`; the first card
  photo on `/stories`; body text on `/team`, `/privacy` and the article. On a 360 px phone the hero
  plush sits below the first screen (top at 669 px in a 590 px screen).

## 7. New findings that are not page defects

### 7.1 The store renders in Washington, D.C. for Indian visitors

Every store response carries `x-vercel-id` with two regions: the edge, then **`iad1`** (Vercel's
US East default), for example `bom1::iad1::…` at 02:48 UTC and `sfo1::iad1::…` at 05:00 UTC. The
marketing pages are prerendered and served from the edge cache (`x-vercel-cache: HIT`, one region).
The archive tag sets no function region (`vercel.json` holds only a cron; no `preferredRegion` in
`src/`). A Mumbai visitor's store page therefore waits on a round trip to the US East coast, plus
whatever the page asks of the database. This fits PostHog's store phone LCP of 3,244 ms with FCP
equal to LCP. Moving the region is a deployment setting, not checkout logic, but it should sit next
to the database: the Supabase region is not recorded in this wiki.

### 7.2 The CSP does not list the Meta gateway the pixel sends to

The pixel's config (`connect.facebook.net/signals/config/1051265191046395`) sets an `openbridge`
endpoint, `https://dv-c3e594c6d429469e90b54478358619c3.ecs.us-east-1.on.aws/`, with a fallback on
`*.us-central1.run.app`. The browser sent 68 `/events` requests there across 57 of the 60
Lighthouse loads; 57 answered 200 (54 from the AWS endpoint, 3 from the fallback). Neither host
is in the CSP `connect-src`, so today it only produces a Report-Only report and the
`inspector-issues` failure. **If the CSP is ever enforced unchanged, these requests
stop.** This is most likely the channel behind the "server" copies Meta builds from browser events
([tracking inventory, section 4](../site/tracking-inventory.md#4-meta-pixel-browser)); that link is
an inference. It belongs on the parity checklist and in C10.

### 7.3 Meta's automatic matching sends our own support number as the visitor's phone

On every marketing page whose PageView went as a GET (all 7 such pages: `/safety`, `/stories`,
`/team`, `/playos`, `/privacy`, `/ai-toys-for-kids-in-india` and the article, on both form factors:
39 of 39 requests), the pixel request carries `udwv[ph][0][v]=b65c555c…`. That is the SHA-256 of
`919187546483`, Kheelona's WhatsApp support number (hash computed 2026-10-11). Home and the product
page send the PageView as a POST, whose body the reports do not show. So Meta is told, for most
visitors, that their phone is our support line.

Where the pixel reads it from is an inference: the number sits in every marketing page's JSON-LD
(`contactPoint.telephone: "+91 91875 46483"`) and in the visible text of only a few (for example
`/privacy`); the store, which shows the number only inside a `wa.me` link and has no JSON-LD, sends
no phone. Automatic advanced matching is on by a founder decision (2026-09-02) and consent is G6;
this is new evidence about what it sends, not a re-ask. The effect on Meta's match quality is also
an inference.
