# D024: Payment methods and GST

**Confirmed by the founder (2026-10-10):**
- Payment methods enabled on Razorpay: **UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking.** Wallets, EMI
  and Pay Later are not offered, so the site never mentions them.
- Every buyer gets a **GST invoice**.
- **Prices include GST** (brief 9.1 and the E-Commerce Rules: total price inclusive of taxes).

**EXTERNAL draft line (final sign-off at Gate A, D015):** next to the CTA, "Pay by UPI, card or net
banking · Price includes GST · GST invoice with every order". Every price shows "incl. GST" where
it appears; the Product JSON-LD price stays the GST-inclusive amount.
