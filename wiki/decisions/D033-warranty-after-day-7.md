# D033: Warranty remedy after the first 7 days (L5, F1 remainder)

**Context.** [D022](D022-warranty-replacement-refund.md) set a 6-month warranty on manufacturing
defects and replacement if a unit is faulty within 7 days of receipt, but left open the remedy for a
defect found later and who pays the pickup. E-Commerce Rule 7(1)(a) requires accurate information on
warranty and the cost of return shipping ([legal review](../product/legal-requirements.md) H2).

**Choice.**
- A manufacturing defect found after the first 7 days and within 6 months: **we repair it, or replace
  it if the repair fails.**
- **Pickup is free** for warranty claims, faulty-on-arrival units and returns we accept.

**What the copy still needs (legal review H2; drafted by the CTO, approved by the founder under
D029).** Rule 7(4) says a seller may not refuse to take back or refund goods that are defective or not
as described, so the policy pages also say: "Faulty or not as described? You can have a replacement or
a full refund." The exchange line defaults to "No exchanges: return it and order again" unless the
founder rules otherwise at Gate A.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
