# D032: Grievance block and a number that takes calls (L2)

**Context.** [D029](D029-grievance-officer-legal-review.md) named the grievance officer and proposed
"acknowledge within 5 days, resolve within 30 days". The [legal review](../product/legal-requirements.md)
(B2, H1) found that E-Commerce Rule 4(5) requires acknowledgement within 48 hours and redress within
one month, and that Rule 4(2)(d) names telephone numbers for customer care and the grievance officer,
while the live WhatsApp number "takes messages, not calls".

**Options put to the founder.** (a) The WhatsApp number takes calls too; (b) a separate call number;
(c) email only for now (a gap against Rule 4(2)(d)).

**Choice: (a).**
- Published wording (the review's text): "Grievance Officer: Aman Soni, Co-Founder and CTO,
  aman@kheelona.com, +91 91875 46483. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it
  within one month of receiving it."
- **+91 91875 46483 takes calls as well as messages.** The "messages only" label is retired on the
  new site; the support row on [facts](../product/facts.md#support-channel) changes with it. Making the
  line answer calls is the founder's to arrange before Gate B; the copy must not go live before.
- "5 days" is never published. Auto-acknowledgement with a complaint reference is recommended
  (review B2) and is operational, not asked ([D030](D030-questions-scoped-to-the-website.md)).
- Where it is shown: /contact, /refund, /terms, /privacy and the store footer, from one content value
  (review H1).

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
