# D066: The founder's answers on the built selling update

## Context

The selling update (D064, D065; [plan v2](../site/d2c-conversion-plan-2026-10-15.md)) was built while the founder slept,
with main-session rulings where the plan left a choice to the founder, and a consistency review found more choices
([CMO review](../qa/cmo-review-selling-update-built-2026-10-15.md)). The founder asked for questions one by one.

## The founder's answers

1. **The WhatsApp bubble on Home's first phone screen** (plan §13 item 7): **after the hero.** The bubble appears with
   the phone buy bar once the hero's button leaves the screen, so the first phone screen has one action, Pre-order. It
   stays on every page (D050), and this overturns the main session's overnight ruling to keep it from first paint.
2. **The WhatsApp share text** (plan §1.7): **signed.** Token mode ends "{price:headline} in total; {price:token} today
   reserves yours, refundable."; full mode "{price:full} in total, paid in full when you order." No "incl. GST". This
   replaces D061 item 7's price clause.
3. **One more button on the product page** (CMO-8): **add it.** A "Pre-order Kheelu" CTA block (price line, risk-reversal
   line) right after the comparison table, with a new tracking value.
4. **Three wordings from the review:** **only "See how Kheelu works"** is approved, under every close (the journal said
   "See how it works"). **Not approved:** the offer lead stays "There every day at home, with no screen to switch off."
   (CMO-7 declined), and the closes without an eyebrow stay without one (CON-6 declined).
5. **F7, after the 6 months of Kheelona+:** **not decided yet.** The site keeps "6 months of Kheelona+ included. Nothing
   renews without you." Asked again before Gate B.
6. **G29, "Tested before launch by trying to make it say unsafe things":** **not true; remove it** everywhere it appears
   (the copy's ready fallbacks). "When it doesn't know something, it says so" stays (D065 item 8).
7. **G28's other half:** **both true.** "Between conversations nothing is sent anywhere, and when Kheelu rests, its Wi‑Fi
   is switched off." and that a single conversation cannot run on without end go on `/safety` and the product page.
8. **Home's length** (R1, about 18 phone screens, the offer at about screen 9): **keep it.** A check stops it growing;
   after launch, PostHog shows how far phone visitors scroll before anything changes.
9. **G27, `/setup`'s two deletion sentences:** **keep them off.**

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-16.
