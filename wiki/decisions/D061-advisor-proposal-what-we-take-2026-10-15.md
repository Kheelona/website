# D061: The advisor's homepage proposal: what we take

## Context

The founder shared a senior marketing advisor's homepage proposal (a content document and a clickable design,
12 October 2026) and asked us to review it against the current site and what makes parents buy, decide what to take,
then plan and update the site. Six specialists reviewed it, the CMO agent decided each of its 37 items and two
challengers tested the decisions ([the review](../research/advisor-homepage-proposal-review.md); the CMO's page
[cmo-review-advisor-proposal-2026-10-15](../qa/cmo-review-advisor-proposal-2026-10-15.md)). Four questions were the
founder's; the founder answered each.

## Choices (founder)

1. **Kheelu on the first phone screen, on Home and the product page.** On phones only, a picture of Kheelu comes into
   the first screen with the approved headline (D045), button and price sentence (D028); laptops unchanged. Built within
   the design system; a change to the approved pages the founder signs (D015, D046). Evidence: on the live site, paid
   phone visitors had 410 sessions and 0 orders, and 90% of their Home views never scrolled; at 390 × 844 only the top
   edge of the art board shows today.
2. **The Meta ads keep landing on Home** (not the product page, not a new ad page). So the Home first phone screen is
   the priority, and no `/lp/meet-kheelu` page is built (D041's "no new pages in v1" stands).
3. **The later price beside ₹4,999 on the offer cards**, as a plain line, only while the first-500 price is live:
   "₹4,999 for the first 500 units. ₹7,999 once they are gone." On the Home and product-page offer cards; no
   strike-through (not lawful until ₹7,999 has been charged, PRICE-01), no counter, no "save". This changes D041's
   "first 500 once, in the price FAQ": Home then says it twice. The paid count stays on file as proof (DARK-01).
4. **Parents' own words from the consented films**, as short written quotes, word for word from the burned-in
   subtitles, each with "Watch the film": Nikita (film "In a parent's words") and Siddhant's father (film "A father's
   view"). The founder confirmed their written consent covers quoting their words in text. The advisor's three
   drafted testimonials are never used (D009).

## Main-session rulings (from the reviews; for the founder's information, each change signed as a deviation row)

- **Taken, in our words:** the "Recognised by" strip (the /team strip as built, D054 item 20) on Home after the films
  and on the product page; four plain steps on the product page with the parent's control on the memory step (D036);
  the labelled example conversation (home.md fold 2 variant b) on the product page; a "Share Kheelu on WhatsApp" link
  under the offer cards with new share text the founder signs (the old text says "talking friend that teaches" and
  "first 500"); the FAQ question "What if my child doesn't like it?" (answer unchanged).
- **Not taken:** the advisor's palette and fonts (the DS is the only source of visual decisions, brief §4), the brain headline (D016),
  "Ages 3+" (3 to 7), ₹499 in the button label (D028), the offer bar, the in-page menu, a looping hero video (D049),
  the stat tiles (unverified or banned forms), the feelings, day and age tiles, the counsellor video, "coming soon"
  products, cash on delivery and the new form fields (D024; the checkout is frozen, ADR-013), "Ships 20 Oct" (D023),
  15-day returns (D021), Marathi and Punjabi (D058 item 7), the withdrawn app features (D058).
- **Kept as approved:** Home fold 5's heading ("You see every conversation"), not "They're just playing. You read every
  word.": the guardrails audit flagged its covert-monitoring framing (DPDP); the founder can still choose it.
- **Cash on delivery** stays off (D024). Parked, not decided: a post-launch project for in-stock orders if payments
  inside the WhatsApp and Instagram browsers keep failing after the "open in your browser" hint.
- **The claims guard** gains the stale facts the advisor's document carried (cash on delivery, "ships 20 Oct",
  open-ended ages, a part-price in a button label, "Real pilot homes", "Backed by", "Safety certified").
- **A fact pack for the advisor** ([for the advisor](../brand/for-the-advisor.md)) before any more work from them.

## Follow-up answers (founder, 2026-10-15, after the build started)

5. **The first-screen picture:** the approved hero art's Kheelu (the plush on a brand-colour block), moved up on
   phones; not a photo with a child, not a film still.
6. **No label line** above the headline on the first screen.
7. **The share text, approved as written:** "Kheelu is a screen-free talking toy for children aged 3 to 7. It answers
   every why, then asks one back. {price:headline} (incl. GST); pre-order with {price:token} today, refundable until
   dispatch." plus the kheelona.com link.
8. **No cash on delivery**; revisit after launch only if payments inside the WhatsApp and Instagram browsers keep
   failing after the "open in your browser" hint.
9. **Home fold 5 keeps its approved heading** ("You see every conversation.").
10. **Made in India** (confirms D034): already in the footer and the product page's "Country of origin"; also as a
    perk on both offer cards (task 8c.5, after 8c.2 merges).
11. **Nothing else comes back** (no counsellor video, no statistics tiles, no "coming soon").
