# D063: Quick batch 1 choices (founder, 2026-10-15)

The first batch under D062. The founder's list is in [the summary](../engineering/project-summary.md) §8; the build is
"Quick batch 1" in [tasks](../engineering/tasks.md). Choices that are the founder's:

1. **Home's hero picture: B2 "Advik · studio hug"** (design-system photo library, `advik-03-studio-hug`: a child hugging
   Kheelu on the cream studio sweep), chosen from five kinds of options (wide home scenes, the "why" moment, Kheelu
   alone, real film stills, the imagination frame). The photo library is AI-made and DS-approved; its rule stands: the
   cast is never presented as customers or placed beside a testimonial.
2. **The phone number +91 91875 46483 comes off every selling page** (heroes, offers, help lines, the footer, the store,
   the journal guides) and **stays on `/contact` and the legal pages' seller block**, because the E-Commerce Rules
   (r.4(2)(d); legal review ECOM-01, ECOM-02) require customer-care contact numbers to be displayed. WhatsApp links and
   email stay everywhere. The receipt emails (frozen code, ADR-013) are not the website and keep it.
3. **The GSTIN comes off the website** (footer, store footer, `/contact`, the legal pages, the JSON-LD `taxID`). Our legal
   review lists no rule requiring it on the site; invoices and the receipt emails keep it.
4. **"What is Kheelu?" becomes a sticky-scroll section with the year-by-year content** the founder shared (the design
   system's own age-timeline copy), adapted to the claims rules: "Ages 3+" reads "Ages 3 to 7"; out: "thinking games"
   (games are not a launch feature, D044), "naming a big feeling" (R5), "grows with them" and "at their own pace" (the
   adaptive-learning family), "a tutor" (Kheelu is a toy, never a tutor), "walks into their first classroom" (a learning
   outcome), "remembers the child it met at 3" (chats are kept 36 months, D058). The founder sees the adapted words on beta.

**Approved by.** Founder (items 1-4; item 4's adaptation by the main session under the claims rules). **Date.** 2026-10-15.
