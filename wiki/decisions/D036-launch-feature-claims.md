# D036: Launch feature claims (F8)

> **Narrowed 2026-10-14 by [D058](D058-follow-the-live-code-check-2026-10-14.md):** #5 (a closed content library) and #8 (a new-word count) are withdrawn after the live team's code check; the weekly four-skills report comes off until it ships.

**Context.** [F8](../open-questions.md) listed behaviour claims that were live or in the positioning
but not confirmed. [D017](D017-live-behaviour-claims-confirmed.md) had confirmed the live ones
(answers then asks back, cannot browse, parents read every conversation, one-tap delete); R5 dropped
mood reading; [D027](D027-story-mode-wording.md) dropped interrupt-and-ask.

**Confirmed true when Kheelu ships (founder, 2026-10-11).**
1. A **weekly report** in the parent app on the four thinking skills.
2. Kheelu **remembers earlier conversations** with the child.
3. When unsure, Kheelu **says it doesn't know** rather than guess.
4. **Parent alerts** in the app (what triggers an alert was not specified: copy says "alerts" only
   after the trigger is defined, or names none).
5. Stories and answers come from a **curated, closed content library**.
6. Replies pass an **age-graded safety layer** set to the child's age.
7. **Red-teamed before launch** (safety tested by trying to make it say unsafe things).
8. The app shows a **new-word count**.
- **How the age is set:** a parent enters the child's age in the parent app at setup and can change
  it.

**Copy rules.** Each claim is written as what the product does, never as an outcome for the child
(no learning-outcome claims, [legal review](../product/legal-requirements.md) L7; brain wording per
[D016](D016-brain-wording.md)). "Remembers" sits next to the parent's controls (read, delete; 36
months, [D020](D020-provider-terms-and-no-sale.md)). Not claimed: "on-device-first processing"
(contradicts [D019](D019-voice-providers.md): conversation goes to outside providers; only Story mode
works offline, D027).

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
