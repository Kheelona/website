# D020: What the voice providers may and may not do

**Confirmed by the founder (2026-10-10):**
1. The outside speech and AI providers ([D019](D019-voice-providers.md)) process audio **only to
   produce Kheelu's reply**.
2. They **do not keep** it.
3. They **do not train** on it.
4. **Contracts** with them say so.
5. **We don't sell your data.** (Already on the live `/privacy`; now confirmed for the new site.)
6. **All processing happens on servers in India**, for every provider (founder, 2026-10-10, option a).
7. **Retention: Kheelona keeps conversation transcripts for 36 months, then deletes them
   automatically**; parents can delete any conversation sooner, in one tap (founder, 2026-10-10, option b).

**EXTERNAL draft lines (the founder signs off final copy at Gate A, D015):**
- "To understand and answer your child, Kheelu uses trusted speech and AI providers, on servers in
  India. They process your child's voice only to produce Kheelu's reply. They don't keep it, and they never use it to
  train their AI. Our contracts with them say so."
- "We don't sell your data."
- Retention: "Your child's conversations stay in your parent app for 36 months, then they're
  deleted automatically. You can delete any of them sooner, in one tap."
- The same 36-month period goes on `/privacy` as the DPDP retention line; counsel review of the
  wording stays with F2.
- Safety block order: the wake-up button and light ([D018](D018-wake-up-button.md)) → the
  providers line → parents read and delete every conversation in the app ([D017](D017-live-behaviour-claims-confirmed.md))
  → we don't sell your data.

**Wording guardrails.** "Trusted" describes our choice of provider, not a certification. "On servers in India"
holds for every provider; if any provider ever moves processing abroad, this line changes first. Conversation
transcripts ARE kept for parents in the app; the "don't keep it" promise is about the providers.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
