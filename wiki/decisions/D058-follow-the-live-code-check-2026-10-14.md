# D058: Follow the live code check; the founder's rulings on what it left open

## Context

On 12 October the live team (Aman) checked every product claim on kheelona.com against the toy's firmware, the backend
and the parent app, withdrew what is not true today (PR #19) and added the parent app to `/privacy` and `/terms` for the
App Store and Play (PR #18). The rebuild still made several of the withdrawn claims, each resting on an earlier decision
of the founder's ([reconciliation map](../qa/live-main-reconciliation-2026-10-14.md); [open questions](../open-questions.md)
G24). The founder answered in three rounds on 2026-10-14; each answer below is theirs.

## Choices

1. **Follow the code check.** "What Aman has pushed [is] correct; follow that." Wherever the live team's code-checked
   wording contradicts an earlier decision, the site uses the code-checked fact. This supersedes, where they conflict:
   D017 and D020 item 7 (one-tap delete), D018 ("about a minute" of quiet), D020 item 6 (servers in India), D036 #5 (a
   closed content library), D036 #8 (the new-word count), the topic and quiet-hours controls, and D055 items 13 (the
   account keeps the WiFi name) and 18 (closing the account keeps the data 36 months). In the site's words:
   - removing a chat in the app hides it on that phone; deleting the account deletes the account, the child's profile,
     and the conversations and recordings from our servers;
   - replies are generated by an AI model given child-safety rules and the child's age on every conversation, with
     Google's safety filters on top ("age-graded safety layer" and "closed library" go);
   - no topic controls, no quiet hours, no new-word count; a conversation ends after a couple of minutes of quiet;
     the WiFi network's name stays on the phone; "We attack our own safety layer before every release" goes;
   - what the live team added as true comes in: the daily talk-time limit, Kheelu's WiFi off at rest, conversations
     readable a few minutes after they end, search, the "Save voice recordings" switch, and the app's privacy settings
     (download the child's data, delete the account); and #18's app sections of `/privacy` and `/terms`.
2. **Bluetooth mode stays** (the founder; the live team had left it open).
3. **The parent app is free; Kheelona+ is a paid subscription on top of it.** Which features stay free is not decided,
   so the site lists none: "Every Kheelu comes with 6 months of Kheelona+, our paid subscription, on top of the free
   parent app." (The live `/terms`' "nothing is sold inside it" is not carried over: Kheelona+ is paid.)
4. **The grievance officer stays Aman Soni, aman@kheelona.com** (D029, D032). The parent app's "Contact our grievance
   officer", which opens connect@kheelona.com, is the founder's to change.
5. **Kheelona trains its own model** on what children say (D035 stands), and a parent opts out in the parent app.
6. **The processors are named**, as the live `/privacy` names them (Google Cloud, Google's Gemini, Sarvam AI, Twilio,
   Aiven, Redis, New Relic, Sentry), with the fact that some process data outside India. Supersedes D055 item 16 and
   the "servers in India" line of D020 item 6.
7. **The languages stay as the rebuild has them** (eight named, up to 10 at launch, switching mid-sentence).
8. **The parent app's report follows the app:** every conversation word for word, a daily summary, search, looking
   back over the week or the month. The weekly four-skills report comes off until it ships.
9. **Chats are kept 36 months, then deleted**: the automatic deletion is live by launch, so the retention line stays,
   and the app screenshot showing "keep chats for 36 months" stays.

## Why

The founder's own answers; item 1 follows the live team's code check, which is the latest evidence of what the
product does (the brief: no invented claims).

## What it changes in the build

The live-site reconciliation in [tasks](../engineering/tasks.md) (wave 8b): the copy pages first (copywriter, fix
mode), the CMO's review, then the modules, the machine files and the parity rules; the 20 guides are corrected as part
of their move to the dashboard (D057 item 1). The founder signs the changed lines at Gate B (D015); the legal pages
with D029's sign-off.
