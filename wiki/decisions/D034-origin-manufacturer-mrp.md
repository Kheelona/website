# D034: Origin, manufacturer and MRP (F3, L4)

**Context.** The listing and the box need Legal Metrology declarations, and the E-Commerce Rules and
brief 9.7 need a country of origin and an MRP inclusive of taxes. Nothing may sell above the printed
MRP, and after the first 500 units Kheelu sells at ₹7,999 ([pricing](../product/pricing.md)).

**Choice.**
- **Country of origin: India** (made in India).
- **Manufacturer: Kheelona Robotics Pvt Ltd**, at the registered address in the seller block. The
  Product JSON-LD's `manufacturer` (Kheelona) is now documented by this decision.
- **MRP: ₹7,999** (inclusive of all taxes), the same as the full price after the first 500.

**Still placeholders** (product page declarations block): net quantity, month and year of
manufacture (specs are not final, F4). Consumer-care contact: the support channel on
[facts](../product/facts.md#support-channel).

**Copy note.** Showing MRP ₹7,999 beside the ₹4,999 launch price must follow D028 and the CCPA
dark-pattern guidelines: no countdown or invented scarcity; "MRP" only labels the real MRP.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
