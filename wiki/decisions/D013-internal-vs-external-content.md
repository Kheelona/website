# D013: Internal vs external content

**Context.** [Q4](../open-questions.md) asked for the source documents behind figures in SKM's
positioning (research brief, competitor review v9, 90-day plan, "the deck").

**Founder's answer, 2026-10-10 (close to verbatim).** The site does not need those figures: they are
internal research. Separate internal data from external, and publish only what external clients,
the ICP, need. This is a sales website, not an internal one. Use the positioning document only
where it is relevant.

**Choice.**
1. The website's only audience is the ICP: parents of 3-7 year olds in India (and family buying
   for them), deciding whether to buy Kheelu.
2. Every fact, claim and message in the wiki is either **EXTERNAL** (may appear on the site, in its
   schema, `llms.txt` and ads) or **INTERNAL** (shapes strategy, never published). The boundary and
   its categories are on [content boundary](../site/content-boundary.md).
3. SKM's positioning is an INTERNAL input. It decides who we talk to, the message hierarchy, the
   objections we answer and the tone. Its research figures, personas, competitor intelligence and
   plans are never quoted on the site.
4. Q4 is closed: the four source documents are not needed.
5. The content model built in Phase 4 holds EXTERNAL items only. Copy review and QA check for
   internal material leaking onto the site.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
