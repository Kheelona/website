# D053: Remove the 3-6-9-12 article

## Context

`/stories/screen-time-rules-parents-swear-by` explains Serge Tisseron's 3-6-9-12 screen rule (one of the 18
articles rewritten in wave 5 part C, task 5.17). The CMO review asked it to name his 2020 "3-6-13-18" markers about
artificial intelligence (5.17 M1). The source check of 2026-10-14
([source verification §1a](../research/source-verification-2026-10-14.md)) read those markers on his association's
site: the second is "Pas de jouets connectés avant 6 ans" (no connected toys before 6: AI listens but does not
understand the child), and the page criticises talking plush toys with a Wi-Fi voice box "offered from age three as an
alternative to screens". Kheelu is that category, for ages 3 to 7. Quoting the markers without that line would be a
selective quote; quoting it on a page that pitches Kheelu sets an expert's advice against the product.

## Options (open question P25)

1. Quote the markers in full and drop the article's product line (the working default).
2. Quote them in full, keep the product line and add one signed answer, as B3 does for Common Sense Media.
3. Take the article off the journal.

## Choice

**Option 3: remove the article** (founder, 2026-10-14).

## What it means

- The journal has 20 articles, not 21. The route `/stories/screen-time-rules-parents-swear-by` answers with a
  one-hop permanent redirect to the hub's screen-time topic, `/stories#screen-time`, so links and search results
  that point to it still land on the subject.
- The hub drops the card (a departure from the approved hub mockup, recorded as a parity deviation); the three
  articles that list it as a related guide (busy-hands, gentle-way, data law) take another, keeping every article's
  three related guides and three incoming links; its copy page and module go; the sitemap, `llms.txt`, the URL map
  and the keyword map follow.
- No other page quotes Tisseron. The 3-6-9-12 rule's sources (C34) stay in the source verification as research.
- Built as task 6.5, after the batch running when the founder answered (`wf_cd8260cf-e88`), because two of the
  related lists belong to its tasks 5.20a and 5.20c.
