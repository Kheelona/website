# D029: Grievance officer and legal review

**Confirmed by the founder (2026-10-11):**
- **Grievance officer:** Aman Soni, Co-Founder and CTO, aman@kheelona.com.
- **Response times proposed:** acknowledge within 5 days, resolve within 30 days.
- **Legal pages:** the founder approves privacy, terms, refund and shipping himself, after an
  **independent review against Indian law and practice** run by the CTO's agents (a check against
  the law's own text, not legal advice).

**Conflict raised immediately (customer-visible, legal):** the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce)
Rules, 2020, Rule 4(5), require the grievance officer to acknowledge a consumer complaint within
**48 hours** and redress it within one month. "5 days" would not meet that. The independent review
confirms the rule text from the official source; the founder decides the published wording.

**Review:** `product/legal-requirements.md` (being written): what Indian law requires the site to
publish and where (Consumer Protection Act 2019 and E-Commerce Rules 2020; Legal Metrology
(Packaged Commodities) Rules for e-commerce declarations; the Toys (Quality Control) Order and BIS
marking; DPDP Act 2023 and its Rules, including verifiable parental consent for children's data;
IT Act and Rules; ASCI codes for advertising claims and advertising to children), each checked
against the official text, then the live pages and the new facts (D018-D024) checked against it.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
