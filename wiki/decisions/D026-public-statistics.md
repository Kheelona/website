# D026: Using public statistics

**Choice (founder, 2026-10-10, R6 = c, with conditions).** Public statistics in SKM's positioning
(e.g. "85% of brain development happens by age 6", "2.2 hours of screen time, double the
guideline") may be used as SKM framed them, **on three conditions**:
1. **Independent review, a second proof on top of SKM's.** Every figure is checked against its
   ORIGINAL public source, never against SKM's summary or a secondary article. If the source does
   not say exactly that, the figure is reworded to what the source says, or dropped. SKM's market
   research is never relied on blindly.
2. **Marked and linked on the site.** Every figure carries a marker (superscript number or
   asterisk) linking to its source: title, publisher or authors, year, section or page, URL.
3. **Public only.** SKM's internal survey figures stay INTERNAL ([D013](D013-internal-vs-external-content.md)).

**Conflict recorded, not re-raised:** the design system's selling guide says never sell through
fear (`design-system/project/07-selling.md`). The founder's explicit instruction wins (brief 3:
founder > DS > positioning). Copy keeps the figures factual and sourced, and pairs each with what
a parent can do, so the page informs rather than alarms.

**Work:** the independent verification is in `research/source-verification.md`
(Phase 1 addendum, being written); the Copywriter writes the footnoted lines from its SUPPORTED rows only.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
