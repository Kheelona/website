# D021: Returns policy

**Confirmed by the founder (2026-10-10):**
- **7-day returns, no questions asked.** Reading applied: within 7 days of **delivery** (a
  pre-order cannot start the clock at payment); the founder may correct this.
- **Opened is fine.** Conditions: **no physical damage**, and **every component returned,
  including the box**.
- Purpose, in the founder's words: parents can buy without risk. This makes the return line a
  conversion device next to the CTA (brief 9.1), not only a policy.

**EXTERNAL draft lines (final sign-off at Gate A, D015):**
- Near the CTA: "7-day returns, no questions asked."
- Policy: "Changed your mind? Return Kheelu within 7 days of delivery, no questions asked. Opened
  is fine. Just send back everything that came in the box, undamaged."

**Still open in F1:** warranty length and coverage, the replacement or repair process for a faulty
unit, who pays the return pickup, and how fast the refund lands. Not to be invented.

**Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules:** the full returns, refund and exchange text goes on
`/refund` and next to the CTA, from one content source (Phase 4). The live `/refund` keeps its
"coming soon" wording until Gate B ([D011](D011-production-held-until-gate-b.md)).

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
