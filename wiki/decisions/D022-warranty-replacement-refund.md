# D022: Warranty, faulty units, pickup and refund

**Confirmed by the founder (2026-10-10):**
1. **Warranty: 6 months on manufacturing defects**; not drops, water or misuse.
2. **Faulty unit: replaced if reported within the first 7 days of receipt.** No timeline is
   published (it depends on where in India the parent is).
3. **We arrange the return pickup.** (Who pays is not stated; copy says only "we arrange".)
4. **Refund within 7 working days** of the unit reaching us, if it is eligible for return.

**EXTERNAL draft lines (final sign-off at Gate A, D015):**
- Near the CTA: "7-day returns, no questions asked · 6-month warranty"
- Warranty: "6-month warranty on manufacturing defects. It doesn't cover drops, water damage or
  misuse."
- Faulty unit: "Arrived faulty? Tell us within 7 days of receiving it and we'll replace it."
- Logistics: "We arrange the pickup. Once your Kheelu reaches us and qualifies for return, your
  refund is processed within 7 working days."

**Guardrails.** Never publish a delivery or replacement timeline. Never say "free pickup" unless
the founder confirms we pay. The warranty remedy for a defect found AFTER the first 7 days (repair
or replace) is not stated: the copy says only "6-month warranty on manufacturing defects" until
it is (open in F1). The Product JSON-LD `hasMerchantReturnPolicy` and warranty fields render from
the same content source (Phase 4).

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
