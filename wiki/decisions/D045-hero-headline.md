# D045: Home hero headline

**Context.** The founder rejected the first headline set (D041: "lacks a hook") and then the round-2 default
"Shh. Your child is explaining the moon to Kheelu." ("this needs to change, give me options and i will
choose"). Four options were offered: "Finally, someone to share the whys with."; "Turn 'why?' into 'what
do you think?'"; "You know their whys. Now hear their becauses."; "Kheelu answers every why. Then it asks
one back."

**Choice (founder).** **"Kheelu answers every why. Then it asks one back."** The handwritten (Hand)
accent is on "asks one back." The sub-line is the short one approved on 2026-10-11: "A screen-free plush
toy by Kheelona. It answers your child first, then asks a question back, so they do the thinking." "It
asks. They think." stays as the hero-board sticker and for ads (D038).

**Fit.** At 360 × 590 the h1 ends at 256 px, the CTA at 432 px, the price sentence at 508 px and the
delivery line at 584 px: all on the first phone screen. The headline picker (a mockup-only review tool)
is removed from Home.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
