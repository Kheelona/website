# D025: How the site handles speech and language

**Choice (founder, 2026-10-10, R4 = a, with placement rules):**
- The site may meet parents searching about speech and language (the deepest demand found in
  Phase 1), talking about how conversation builds language.
- **Public sources only, and carefully.** Every statement about speech or language cites a public,
  authoritative source (for example WHO, IAP or AAP guidance, or peer-reviewed research) that the
  Copywriter has actually read. Never internal research ([D013](D013-internal-vs-external-content.md)),
  never an outcome claim for Kheelu ([D016](D016-brain-wording.md)).
- **Disclaimer placement:** on the **Terms** page and on **Meet Kheelu** (`/products/kheelu`, the
  product page; it exists, so not `/playos`). **Not on Home.**
- Disclaimer wording (draft): "Kheelu is a toy, not a therapy or treatment. If you're worried
  about your child's speech, talk to your paediatrician."
- Journal articles that are specifically about speech delay keep a one-line disclaimer inside the
  article (current practice; the founder may say otherwise).

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.
