# D064: Quick batch 2 (founder, 2026-10-15)

Supersedes quick batch 1 (held before anything was built) where they differ; D063 items 2-3 (the phone number and the
GSTIN) stand, item 1 (the hero photo) and item 4 (the year-by-year section) are replaced below. Built under D062.

1. **Home's films ("Kheelu asks. Watch them think."):** the 2nd film replaced by the 4th and the 3rd by the 7th.
2. **Safety:** Home's "Safety you can check." and "You see every conversation." merge into **one compact block on Home**
   that points to `/safety` (where the detail already lives). The product page's safety sections move to `/safety`
   (duplicates removed there); **the product page keeps only a link to `/safety`**.
3. **The WhatsApp logo** from Wikimedia Commons' WhatsApp.svg, adapted as needed.
4. **Home's hero picture: A3 "Kabir · block rocket"** (photo library `kabir-02-block-rocket`), cropped. **Home only**: the
   other pages keep their illustrated heroes.
5. **"What you get, and what you pay.":** the girl's picture aligned with the offer card.
6. **The footer's link gaps:** inspected and tightened.
7. **"What is Kheelu?" redesigned:** a sticky-scroll section with 4-6 points that tell a parent what it is and why to
   buy it; re-planned and re-written (copywriter, then the CMO, then the claims guard).
8. **The expert clip moves to Home**, right after "What is Kheelu?", and leaves the product page. The burned-in
   disclosure and **the burned-in captions are removed** (the founder: remove captions entirely). The page's disclosure
   line under the credit stays (she was compensated, D061 item 12). Accepted risk, the founder's: the clip has no
   captions, against the design system's accessibility rule (DS 08); parents watching on mute won't follow her words.
9. **A languages section on Home** ("Kheelu speaks up to 10 languages", with "at launch" as every surface says it,
   D058 item 7) after "Conversation helps a young brain grow." and before "What is Kheelu?"; the languages leave the
   bottom of "What is Kheelu?".
10. **Motion across the site:** the founder picked three patterns on top of D049's base (the scroll fade-and-rise stays;
    the hero stays still): **line drawing** (each heading's one accent writes or draws itself as it appears, because the
    DS allows one Hand phrase or one Mark per heading, never both, 10-pages.md:39; the chalk doodles draw themselves as
    today), **micro-interactions** (the button press and arrow nudge as today, cards lift a little on hover, icons nudge,
    links' underlines slide in, the FAQ plus turns), and **the sticky-scroll story** (only in "What is Kheelu?": a
    progress line fills and the picture changes with each point). Not taken: sticker pop. Same everywhere, once per
    element, never looping, transform and opacity only (the FAQ's open as before), nothing for reduced motion.
11. **The GSTIN off the site and the phone number only on `/contact` and the legal pages** (D063 items 2-3), confirmed.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-15.
