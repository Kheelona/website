# D027: Story mode wording

**Founder, 2026-10-10 (R8 = a):**
- **"Lessons" is dropped everywhere** (Kheelu is not sold as a tutor).
- **Story mode content: stories, poems and jingles.** Preferred direction: "engaging stories,
  poems, jingles, your kids learn from and enjoy".
- **Correction: there is currently NO feature to interrupt a story and ask about it.** The live
  claim "stories and lessons it can interrupt, question and be quizzed on" is BANNED. This narrows
  [D017](D017-live-behaviour-claims-confirmed.md): that live line was never real, so "all live
  behaviour claims are confirmed" excludes it. The application pass re-checks each R1-marked row
  rather than flipping them all.

**EXTERNAL draft line (final sign-off at Gate A, D015):** "**Story mode:** Stories, poems and
jingles your child enjoys and learns from. No WiFi needed." ("No WiFi needed" restates the
verified offline fact.)

**Kheelona+ line (founder chose option b, 2026-10-10):** poems and jingles are part of Kheelona+.
"Every Kheelu comes with 6 months of Kheelona+: stories, poems, jingles, languages and the parent
app. Kheelu's smart features keep working for life, with no subscription needed. Kheelona+
pricing will be announced soon." It replaces `KHEELONA_PLUS_LINE` on the new site (the live
constant is untouched until Gate B, D011) and answers R10's Kheelona+ half: "keep working for
life, with no subscription needed" cannot be read as a free lifetime subscription.
