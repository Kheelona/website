# D065: The site sells (founder, 2026-10-15)

The founder's feedback: the site informs but lacks clear CTAs and what a client needs to convert; plan it as a D2C brand
that sells. The copywriter's end-to-end plan follows (wiki/site/d2c-conversion-plan-2026-10-15.md). The founder's answers:

1. **The promise, direct and within D016:** "Screen-free AI toy · Ages 3 to 7" over "The AI toy that answers every why,
   then asks your child one back." Brain development stays a journal topic (D016 unchanged).
2. **Ages 3 to 7** everywhere (D009 unchanged; never "Ages 3+").
3. **The button stays "Pre-order Kheelu"** with a short price line beside it ("₹499 today, refundable. ₹4,999 in total." or
   as short); **GST and the payment breakdown only at checkout** (the store and `/thanks`): "no need to mention about GST
   and other technical things at all places... We are unnecessarily overwhelming the user." Changes D028's long sentence
   on the selling pages; the store keeps the full sentence and the breakdown.
4. **"First 500" stays on the offer cards only** (D061 item 3); no urgency line near the top.
5. **G28 answered: the daily talk-time limit is true and live:** the site may say each Kheelu has a daily limit on how long
   it talks.
6. **G30 answered: Kheelona+ never starts charging without the parent's own sign-up:** "Nothing renews without you." may
   sit beside the price and in the FAQ.
7. **G26 answered: "Delete all chats" now deletes the chats from the servers** (the app changed after the live team's code
   check of 12 Oct, D058 item 1): the privacy-centre screenshot stays as it is; the site may say a parent can delete all
   chats from the parent app. The claims guard and the claims register follow this answer.
8. **G29 answered: "When Kheelu doesn't know something, it says so instead of guessing"** is how the model behaves; the
   line stays.
