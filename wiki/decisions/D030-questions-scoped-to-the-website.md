# D030: Questions scoped to the website draft

**Context.** After the founder answered L1 (BIS licence under application), the CTO asked what
happens to the 20 Oct dispatch of existing token orders, because the
[legal review](../product/legal-requirements.md) (BIS-01) reads the Toys QCO as barring dispatch of
an unmarked toy.

**Founder's reply (2026-10-11):** "this question is not a part of current website draft plan.
focus on task in hand".

**Choice.** Ask the founder only what the website draft needs: copy, claims, published policies,
page and schema facts, tracking and Gate A decisions. Operational matters (dispatch timing,
licensing timelines, logistics, balance-payment handling, product setup flows) are not asked.
Where an operational fact shapes copy, the site uses the safe default and says nothing it cannot
support.

**What this means for the open items.**
- L1: the site claims no BIS licence or ISI mark until a licence number exists (BIS-02); the honest
  status is "applied for, not yet granted". Delivery copy for new orders follows
  [D023](D023-new-order-delivery.md) (mid-November) and never states a date that depends on the
  licence. The dispatch question is closed as out of scope.
- Out of scope, not asked: the operational parts of F12 (existing-order dispatch, balance links,
  WhatsApp confirmations, balance measurement), L3 (charger and battery registration, WPC: the
  site simply claims none), the setup-flow consent part of L6, and the dated duties in L8.
- Still asked, because they land on pages: L2 (grievance wording), L4/F3 (declarations on the
  product page), L5 (returns wording), L6's privacy-policy wording, F15 (the date in Product
  JSON-LD), F16, F17, C11, C13 and the Gate A items.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
