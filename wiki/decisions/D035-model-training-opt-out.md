# D035: Model training (F16)

**Context.** The live /playos page says the model "learns from conversations families separately opt
into", while [D020](D020-provider-terms-and-no-sale.md) promises the outside providers never train on
a child's voice.

**Founder's answers (2026-10-11).**
1. "Everyone goes to training, parents can opt out. You can mention this in terms and privacy. I don't
   think it is needed on customer pages."
2. What trains: **voice recordings and transcripts**, used by **Kheelona's own model**. The outside
   providers still never train (D020 holds).
3. Default: **opt-out, kept** (offered: opt-in from launch; opt-in before the DPDP duties start).

**What this means for the site.**
- **Terms and privacy** state it plainly: what is used (voice and transcripts), by whom (Kheelona's
  own model), why, that it is on by default, how a parent turns it off, and the 36-month retention
  ([D020](D020-provider-terms-and-no-sale.md)).
- **Customer pages** (Home, product page, Meet Kheelu, FAQ) do not mention training, by the founder's
  choice. They must also never imply the opposite: no "never used to train AI", "your child's voice is
  never used", or "we keep nothing". D020's line stays scoped to the providers ("our speech and AI
  providers ... never train on it"). The live /playos opt-in line is retired (it is false under D035).
- The 36-month retention and the training use go together on the privacy page.

**Risk recorded with the decision (a reading of the law's text, not legal advice; counsel to
confirm).** DPDP Act s.6(1) requires consent by "a clear affirmative action", and s.9(1) verifiable
consent of a parent before processing a child's personal data. An opt-out default is unlikely to meet
that once the DPDP duties apply (13 May 2027 per the [legal review](../product/legal-requirements.md);
the date is for counsel to confirm). Leaving training off customer pages while the trust copy talks
about voice handling also carries a misleading-by-omission risk (CPA s.2(28)), which is why the
customer-page wording above stays scoped. The founder chose opt-out knowing this.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-11.
