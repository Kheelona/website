# D016: How the site talks about the brain

**Choice (founder, 2026-10-10, option b).**
- **Every promise follows the founder's 2026-10-04 rule:** conversation helps a young brain grow,
  and Kheelu gives a child more of it. Never "Kheelu grows / helps grow / develops your child's
  brain", and the product is never called the thing that develops the brain.
- **"Brain development" may be used as a TOPIC** in titles, headings and articles so the site is
  found in search ("brain development toys": Keyword Planner India 1k-10k a month, read
  2026-09-05). Example: "Brain development at 3 to 7: why talking matters most".
- This settles the six lines under R7: SKM's hero and "Kheelu helps your child's brain grow" stay
  BANNED; "brain-development toy" as a product label is BANNED, but usable as a searched topic
  phrase; "Built to support your child's brain development" and "a gift that grows young minds"
  must be reworded to the conversation form; "so they think, talk and wonder more" is allowed when
  it describes what conversation does.
