# D016: How the site talks about the brain

**Choice (founder, 2026-10-10, option b).**
- **Every promise follows the founder's 2026-10-04 rule:** conversation helps a young brain grow,
  and Kheelu gives a child more of it. Never "Kheelu grows / helps grow / develops your child's
  brain", and the product is never called the thing that develops the brain.
- **"Brain development" may be used as a TOPIC** in titles, headings and articles so the site is
  found in search ("brain development toys": Keyword Planner India 1k-10k a month, read
  2026-09-05). Example: "Brain development at 3 to 7: why talking matters most".
- This settles the six lines under R7: SKM's hero and "Kheelu helps your child's brain grow" stay
  BANNED; "brain-development toy" as a product label is BANNED, but usable as a searched topic
  phrase; "Built to support your child's brain development" and "a gift that grows young minds"
  must be reworded to the conversation form; "so they think, talk and wonder more" is allowed when
  it describes what conversation does.

**To apply:** claims.md, messaging.md and positioning.md after the running completion pass
(`wf_08ea6a36-c38`) finishes; the Copywriter follows this in Phase 3.

**Approved by.** Founder. **Date.** 2026-10-10.

**Superseded in part by [D070](D070-conversion-bug-fixes-2026-11-24.md) / B7a (2026-11-24).** The founder's chat of
10 November rejected "An AI toy that supports brain development." (the home H1), "Talking helps young brains grow ...
Khelu is built on the same idea" (the home chapter above the offer) and "We make AI toys that develop brains." (the footer
of every page): no developmental, cognitive or brain-outcome claim is made without proof the founder approves. The
"conversation helps a young brain grow" form above is therefore no longer used on the home page or the footer, and the
site's promises describe what Khelu does (it answers a child's questions, then asks one back), not what it does to the
brain. B7a replaced those lines and `test/guards/banned-phrases.data.ts` bans them
([claims §4](../product/claims.md#4-retired-or-banned-wording)). "Brain development" as a searched TOPIC in titles,
headings and articles (the second bullet) is unchanged.
