# D018: The wake-up button replaces the wake word

**New product fact (founder, 2026-10-10).** Kheelu has **no wake word**. It has a **wake-up
button**; the microphone is on only when someone physically presses it. This supersedes R2 and
both earlier wordings.

**Wording rules for all copy, schema and `llms.txt`:**
- BANNED from now on: every wake-word line, i.e. the retired "The microphone is off. Not muted.
  Off." family AND the 2026-10-04 replacement "listens only for its wake word, and records or
  sends nothing before it", plus "Hey Kheelu" style prompts, anywhere they imply a wake word.
- Describe the mechanism, not an absolute: say "the microphone stays off until someone presses
  the button, so nothing is recorded or sent". Do NOT say "no risk" or "zero risk" (an absolute
  safety claim needs substantiation under ASCI). Do not claim the microphone is physically or
  electrically disconnected unless the founder confirms it.
- The button is the **wake-up button** (en-IN, hyphenated).

**How the button works (founder, 2026-10-10, option b).** One press starts a conversation: the
microphone stays on through the back-and-forth and **switches itself off after about a minute
with no conversation** ("like 1 min": write "about a minute"). The next conversation needs
another press. **A light on Kheelu shows while the microphone is on** (founder, 2026-10-10;
colour and pattern not specified, so copy never names one).

**Draft lines (EXTERNAL; the founder signs off final copy at Gate A, D015):**
- Short: "No wake word. Press to talk. Off after a minute of quiet."
- Safety heading: "You'll always see when Kheelu is listening." (alternative: "Kheelu only
  listens when someone presses its button.")
- Light line: "A light on Kheelu shows whenever it's listening."
- Body: "Kheelu has no wake word, so it never listens for one. Its microphone turns on only when
  someone presses the wake-up button. It stays on while your child talks and switches itself off
  after about a minute of quiet. Left alone, it hears nothing and sends nothing."
- FAQ "Is Kheelu always listening?": "No. Kheelu has no wake word to listen for. Its microphone
  turns on only when someone presses the wake-up button. It stays on while your child is talking
  and switches itself off after about a minute of quiet. Until the next press, nothing is heard,
  recorded or sent."
- Never say "nothing is ever recorded": conversations ARE kept for parents to read in the app
  (D017); the claim is about the off state only.

**To apply:** facts.md (listening row), claims.md (sections 4 and 4.1: the 15 live locations
become "replace with the button wording"), and F4 (the "wake word" spec item is moot), together
with D016 and D017 after the completion run. The design system's wake-word examples are logged
for its owner as a gap.
