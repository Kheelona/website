## Fold 1 · We can't find that page

**Fold job:** say plainly what happened, then give four ways on, each one tap.

**Copy:**

> **Eyebrow:** Page not found
>
> **H1:** We can't find that page.
>
> **Lede:** The link may be old, or the address may have a typo. Where were you headed?
>
> - **[Home]** → `home.html`
>   What Kheelu is, on one page.
> - **[Meet Kheelu]** → `meet-kheelu.html`
>   The toy, its three modes and the price.
> - **[Stories]** → `stories.html`
>   Guides for parents of 3 to 7 year olds.
> - **[Contact]** → `contact.html`
>   WhatsApp, call or email the team.

- **H1 accent:** none (an error page stays plain; DS errors "own it, say what to do", `01-writing.md:92`).
- **Component:** `EmptyState`, composed (the component map names it for the 404 page): icon `magnifying-glass` at `icon-xl` in its 80 px circle, the eyebrow, H1 and lede, then the four links as a list of full-width rows, each at least 44 px tall, with icons `house`, `heart`, `book-open`, `chat-circle-dots`. The link text is the bold label; the line under it is part of the same link (one tap target per row).
- The lede's closing question ("Where were you headed?") echoes the brand's asking-back idea without claiming anything about the toy.
- **Image (optional):** none needed. If a colour moment is wanted, a small board on `kh-tone-blue` with `kheelu-front-sticker.png` and one chalk doodle (`chalk-question-mark.svg`, `alt=""`). Board alt: "Kheelu, the cream plush rabbit, beside a chalk question mark." No cast child.

**H1 options**

| | H1 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **A (recommended)** | We can't find that page. | Owns the miss in five words, as the DS error tone asks |
| B | This page isn't here. | Neutral; does not say whose miss it is |
| C | That link didn't work. | Points at the link, which is often the truth (old ads, old QR codes) |
