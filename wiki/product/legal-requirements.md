# Legal requirements for the website (India)

## What you need to know

1. **Two blockers.** Kheelu may not be imported, sold or dispatched without a BIS licence and the ISI mark (Toys QCO para 3; BIS Act s.17(1), a cognizable offence), and none has been granted; and the approved "acknowledge within 5 days" cannot be published, because E-Commerce Rule 4(5) requires acknowledgement within 48 hours.
2. **Publish the grievance officer now** (missing since launch; Rule 4(4), SPDI Rule 5(9)): "Grievance Officer: Aman Soni, Co-Founder and CTO, aman@kheelona.com. We acknowledge every complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month of receiving it." Add a phone number that takes calls and a complaint reference number.
3. **Before the first unit ships:** post-delivery returns, exchange, return-shipping cost and warranty terms, with a refund option for faulty or not-as-described units (Rules 7(1)(a), 7(4)); Legal Metrology declarations on the listing and the box, with one MRP of at least ₹7,999; BIS registration for the charger and battery; and WPC radio approval (its official text could not be fetched).
4. **Before the first toy connects to our servers:** a product privacy policy that matches D018 to D020 and the signed provider contracts, and a parental-consent step at setup. The DPDP duties (verifiable parental consent, no tracking or behavioural monitoring of children, 72-hour breach notice) bind from 13 May 2027, well inside the 36-month retention period.
5. **Copy:** drop "free" from Kheelona+ and say what stops after 6 months; keep the ₹4,999 total beside every ₹499; let "first 500" follow the live count; no learning-outcome claims and no drafted testimonials; the live wake-word, "microphone is off" and "own voice brain" lines are now false on the founder's own facts.
6. **Counts:** 65 verified rows: 3 blocker rows (2 distinct issues), 25 high, 28 medium, 9 low. One high (WPC-01) rests on text that could not be fetched. 1 January 2027 adds five more E-Commerce duties.

## 1. Blockers and high risks

### B1. No BIS licence or ISI mark for the toy (BIS-01, blocker)

- **Provision.** [Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020][toyqco], S.O. 853(E), para 3: toys "shall
  conform to the corresponding Indian Standard (s) ... and shall bear the Standard Mark under a
  licence from the Bureau"; the Table lists "IS 15644: 2006 Safety of Electric Toys" and IS 9873
  Parts 1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 9. Para 2(a): "for use in play by children under 14 years of age". In force
  since 1 January 2021 ([S.O. 3146(E)][toy-ext]). [BIS Act 2016][bis-act] s.17(1): "No person shall
  manufacture, import, distribute, sell, hire, lease, store or exhibit for sale any such goods ...
  (a) without a Standard Mark, except under a valid licence"; s.29(3): imprisonment up to two years
  or a fine of not less than ₹2 lakh, up to ten times the value of the goods; s.29(4): cognizable.
- **Why it applies.** Kheelu is a plush toy with electronics for children aged 3 to 7, so it is a
  toy and an electric toy. The live site says "no toy-safety certificate has been issued to us".
  The only exemptions are export, registered artisans and GI proprietors, and up to 300 R&D samples
  a year not for sale ([S.O. 4277(E)][toy2024]).
- **Founder action.** No import or dispatch of any unit until the licence covering IS 15644 (and the
  applicable IS 9873 parts) is granted and the ISI mark with licence number is on the product. A
  factory outside India holds the licence itself, or use the DPIIT permission route in the
  [Transition Facilitation QCO 2026][trans-qco] (applications within 24 months of 25 June 2026). If
  the licence will not be in hand by the ship date, tell pre-order holders and offer the full
  refund the terms already promise. Counsel advised: is taking paid pre-orders for an unmarked toy
  "exhibit for sale" under s.17(1)? No ship-date copy may promise a date that depends on an
  unissued licence.

### B2. "Acknowledge within 5 days" breaches the 48-hour rule (ECOM-03 and DATA-03, blocker)

- **Provision.** [E-Commerce Rules 2020][ecom2020] Rule 4(5): "Every e-commerce entity shall ensure
  that the grievance officer ... acknowledges the receipt of any consumer complaint within
  forty-eight hours and redresses the complaint within one month from the date of receipt of the
  complaint." In force since 23 July 2020. From 1 January 2027 ([G.S.R. 789(E)][ecom2026]) the
  officer must also give the complainant "a copy of the complaint as recorded". SPDI Rule 5(9)
  ([print][spdi-print]) sets no acknowledgement time but caps redress at one month; from 13 May
  2027 DPDP Rule 14(3) ([Rules][dpdp-rules]) requires the response period to be published, at most
  ninety days.
- **Why it applies.** Rule 4(5) has no size or volume exception. A published "5 days" commits the
  team to a process that breaches the rule on every complaint acknowledged after hour 48.
  "Resolve within 30 days" also differs from "one month": a complaint received on 1 February 2027
  has one month to 1 March, but 30 days to 3 March (the General Clauses Act reading of "month" is
  UNVERIFIED here, U2).
- **Founder action.** Replace the line approved in this run with: **"We acknowledge every complaint
  within 48 hours and resolve it within one month of receiving it."** (or "within 28 days" if a day
  count is wanted). Set email and WhatsApp to auto-acknowledge with a complaint reference, so 48
  hours holds over weekends. From 1 January 2027 attach the complaint as recorded. Nothing goes out
  with "5 days". The name, designation and email the founder approved are fine.

### H1. No grievance officer on the site (ECOM-02, DATA-02, high)

- **Provision.** Rule 4(4): "shall appoint a grievance officer for consumer grievance redressal,
  and shall display the name, contact details, and designation of such officer on its platform";
  Rule 4(2)(d) names "e-mail address, fax, landline and mobile numbers of customer care as well as
  of grievance officer" (fax dropped from 1 January 2027). SPDI Rule 5(9): "designate a Grievance
  Officer and publish his name and contact details on its website".
- **Why it applies.** Kheelona sells its own stock on its own site (Rule 3(1)(f) inventory
  e-commerce entity) and collects personal data today. The word "grievance" appears on no page.
- **Founder action.** Publish the block in B2 on /contact, /refund, /terms, /privacy and the store
  footer, beside the seller block, from one content value. Add a phone number for the officer and
  customer care that takes calls (the live WhatsApp "takes messages, not calls"; Rule 4(2)(d)
  names landline and mobile numbers).

### H2. No post-delivery terms, and a replacement-only remedy for faulty units (ECOM-05, ECOM-06, high)

- **Provision.** Rule 7(1)(a): "accurate information related to return, refund, exchange, warranty
  and guarantee, delivery and shipment, cost of return shipping, mode of payments, grievance
  redressal mechanism". Rule 7(4): "No inventory e-commerce entity shall refuse to take back goods
  ... or refuse to refund consideration, if paid, if such goods ... are defective ... or ... not of
  the characteristics or features as advertised ... or ... delivered late from the stated delivery
  schedule" (force majeure excuses lateness only). [CPA][cpa] s.2(47)(viii): refusing to take back
  defective goods and refund "within the period stipulated in the bill ... or in the absence of
  such stipulation, within a period of thirty days".
- **Why it applies.** Live /refund: returns and warranty terms "are not written yet". The planned
  D022 line ("Arrived faulty? Tell us within 7 days of receiving it and we'll replace it.") offers
  replacement only, and D021's conditions were written for change-of-mind returns.
- **Founder action.** Before the first dispatch: "Faulty or not as described? You can have a
  replacement or a full refund."; D021 conditions for change-of-mind returns only; the remedy for a
  defect found after day 7 (F1); who pays return pickup, stated as an amount or "free"; an exchange
  line (even "no exchanges; return and re-order"); a defect period printed on the invoice.

### H3. Delivery date shown to new buyers is wrong for their order (ECOM-07, high)

- **Provision.** Rule 7(1)(a) ("accurate information related to ... delivery and shipment"); CPA
  s.2(28)(iv) (an advertisement that "deliberately conceals important information").
- **Why it applies.** Every page and the store show "20 October 2026"; new orders deliver from
  mid-November ([D023](../decisions/D023-new-order-delivery.md)).
- **Founder action.** Recorded only: D023 keeps the live date until Gate B, an accepted risk, not
  reopened here. The live terms already allow cancellation for a full refund if the date moves,
  which matches Rule 7(4). The new site renders "Deliveries start mid-November" from one value and
  needs an exact date for JSON-LD (F15).

### H4. Origin, importer, Legal Metrology declarations and MRP (ECOM-11, LM-01, LM-02, high)

- **Provision.** [LM (Packaged Commodities) Rules 2011][lm2011] Rule 6(10) ([2017][lm2017]): "An
  E-Commerce entity shall ensure that the mandatory declarations as specified in sub-rule (1),
  except the month and year ... shall be displayed on the digital and electronic network used for
  e-commerce transactions". Rule 6(1): maker, packer or importer name and address; country of
  origin for imports; generic name; net quantity (by number); retail sale price; dimensions. Rule
  2(m): MRP is "inclusive of all taxes". Rule 18(2): no sale "at a price exceeding the retail sale
  price". E-Commerce Rule 4(6): "mention the name and details of any importer".
- **Why it applies.** Kheelu is a pre-packed commodity sold by number on Kheelona's own site. No
  page shows a maker, importer, origin, net quantity or MRP; /contact says "The toy is designed and
  built here", which is itself a claim (CPA s.2(28)(i)) if any part is made abroad.
- **Founder action.** Answer F3 (where made and assembled; importer of record). Publish a
  declarations block next to the price and print the same on the box. Set one MRP of at least
  ₹7,999 (incl. of all taxes) on every box, because Rule 18(2) bars selling above the printed MRP
  and a sticker may only lower it (Rule 6(3) proviso). Correct "built here" unless literally true.

### H5. Charger and battery need BIS registration (BIS-03, high)

- **Provision.** [CRS Order 2021][crs2021] para 2: listed goods "shall bear the 'Standard' Mark under
  a license ... as per Scheme-II"; Schedule items 16 and 17 (power adaptors, now IS/IEC 62368-1:2023
  per the [Scheme II list][crs-list]), 20 (sealed secondary cells, IS 16046), 42 (adapters, IS 302-1).
- **Why it applies.** /products/kheelu lists "A charger." in the box; Kheelu is battery powered. No
  R-number is known.
- **Founder action.** Get the R-numbers from the manufacturer, or ship a cable only. Ask the BIS
  lab whether the internal battery is covered by the toy licence or needs its own registration
  (the Order text does not say).

### H6. WiFi and Bluetooth radio approval (WPC-01, high, text UNVERIFIED)

- **Provision.** Not verified: the DoT/WPC Equipment Type Approval instructions returned 404 and 403
  ([tried][eta-1]).
- **Why it applies.** AI mode uses home WiFi and Bluetooth mode pairs a phone, so Kheelu has a
  2.4 GHz radio. The fact pattern is certain; the legal text behind it is not checked.
- **Founder action.** Get the ETA certificate for Kheelu or its radio module from the manufacturer,
  or file the self-declaration ETA (or its successor on the DoT Authorisation Portal under the
  Telecommunications Act, 2023) before import or first dispatch. Counsel or the test lab to confirm
  the current rule.

### H7. Live claims the founder has confirmed are false (ECOM-15, DATA-27, high)

- **Provision.** Rule 7(3): advertisements must be "consistent with the actual characteristics,
  access and usage conditions"; CPA s.2(47)(i)(d) (goods represented as having "characteristics
  ... which such goods ... do not have"), s.21(2) (penalty up to ₹10 lakh, ₹50 lakh after),
  s.89 (up to two years and ₹10 lakh); SPDI Rule 4(1)(i): "Clear and easily accessible statements
  of its practices and policies".
- **Why it applies.** Live: "Your child says the wake word", "the microphone is off. Not muted.
  Off.", "thinks on the device first", "stories and lessons your child can interrupt, question, and
  be quizzed on", "Kheelona's own voice brain", "stays in your region", "Nothing is kept that you
  cannot delete", and the footer on every page "Wake-word listening." The founder's facts say
  otherwise ([D018](../decisions/D018-wake-up-button.md), [D019](../decisions/D019-voice-providers.md),
  [D020](../decisions/D020-provider-terms-and-no-sale.md), [D027](../decisions/D027-story-mode-wording.md)).
  As brand owner Kheelona is a "manufacturer" for s.21 and s.89 (s.2(24)(iii)).
- **Founder action.** D011 holds production until Gate B. The privacy lines become false
  descriptions of real processing the day the first unit connects, so decide whether Gate B, or a
  minimal patch of these lines, comes first. The new site ships D018, D020 and D027 wording only.

### H8. Drafted testimonials on named people (ECOM-16, high)

- **Provision.** Rule 7(2): "No inventory e-commerce entity shall falsely represent itself as a
  consumer and post reviews"; [CCPA ad guidelines][ccpa-ads] para 13(1): an endorsement "must
  reflect the genuine, reasonably current opinion"; para 14: disclose a material connection.
- **Why it applies.** The claims register records the live quotes as "Drafted words, not spoken.
  The names are real; the quotes are not."
- **Founder action.** Recorded only: the founder kept the live quotes (2026-07-28) and the new site
  bans them ([D009](../decisions/D009-brief-readings.md)). For the films: if pilot families got a
  free or discounted unit, say so beside the film.

### H9. Kheelona+ is called "free", and what stops after month 6 is undisclosed (DARK-04, high)

- **Provision.** CCPA ad guidelines para 7(d): a free-claim advertisement shall "not describe an
  element of a package as free if such element is included in the package price"; [Dark Patterns
  Guidelines][dark] Annexure 1(8)(iii)-(iv), 1(4) illustration (a) ("prohibiting a user from
  continuing with the use of product or service for the consideration originally paid ... unless
  they upgrade"), 1(5) subscription trap, 1(12) illustration (a) (no notice when a trial converts).
- **Why it applies.** Live: "Kheelona+: included free for 6 months with every Kheelu; pricing after
  that is announced soon." The parent app, which holds the conversation log and delete control,
  sits inside Kheelona+ (D027), and its post-6-month price and scope are unknown (F7).
- **Founder action.** Remove "free" now. Before Gate A, publish: what keeps working with no
  subscription; what stops after month 6; the price, or a firm statement that it is optional,
  announced before month 6, never auto-renewed; a promise to notify before month 6 ends. Keep the
  log and delete controls outside any paywall (see DATA-20, DATA-22). Never "free for life" (F7).

### H10. Learning and development outcome claims (ADV-01, high)

- **Provision.** CCPA ad guidelines para 8(1)(n): an advertisement that "addresses or targets or
  uses children shall not ... claim that consumption of a product advertised shall have an effect
  on enhancing intelligence or physical ability ... without any valid substantiation or adequate
  scientific evidence"; para 8(1)(c), (k); para 12(b) (source and date of research); [ASCI][asci]
  1.2 and 1.5.
- **Why it applies.** Live: "the talking toy that teaches", "A head start for school", "School
  teaches the class. Kheelu teaches your child.", "grows their vocabulary"; the pages and films use
  children.
- **Founder action.** Ship only the [D016](../decisions/D016-brain-wording.md) form, with source and
  date beside every research-based line ([D026](../decisions/D026-public-statistics.md)); retire the
  outcome lines unless evidence about Kheelu itself is on file (F9).

### H11. No privacy policy for the product, and two data laws in sequence (DATA-00, DATA-01, high)

- **Provision.** SPDI Rule 4(1): the policy "shall be published on website ... and shall provide for
  (i) Clear and easily accessible statements of its practices and policies; (ii) type of personal
  or sensitive personal data ... (iii) purpose ... (iv) disclosure ... (v) reasonable security
  practices". [G.S.R. 843(E)][dpdp-start]: DPDP ss.3 to 17 start "eighteen months from the date of
  publication" (13 November 2025), so 13 May 2027; Act s.44(2) then omits IT Act s.43A and
  s.87(2)(ob).
- **Why it applies.** /privacy covers the pre-order and website only, promises "A full product
  privacy policy will be published here before Kheelu ships", has no security section, and says
  "Five tools do it" then "The fourth is different".
- **Founder action.** Publish the product part of /privacy before any unit connects, written to the
  SPDI Rules now and ready for DPDP (section 4 checklist). Name every tool without a count.

### H12. Provider contracts and the "in India" promise (DATA-19, DATA-26, high)

- **Provision.** DPDP s.8(1): the Data Fiduciary is responsible for processing "on its behalf by a
  Data Processor"; s.8(2): only "under a valid contract"; Rule 6(1)(f) security clauses. SPDI
  Rule 7 limits sensitive data transfers abroad now; DPDP s.16(1) allows transfers unless the
  Government restricts a country.
- **Why it applies.** The planned D020 line says providers process "only to produce Kheelu's
  reply", "don't keep it", "never use it to train", "on servers in India", and "Our contracts with
  them say so". Store functions run in Vercel iad1 (US East); Supabase's region is unknown (C17);
  PostHog stores in the US.
- **Founder action.** Before Gate A, check each signed provider contract clause by clause against
  that line (including failover, support access and sub-processors), and get a written region map
  of every component touching child data (providers, transcript store, app backend, logs,
  backups). Publish only what both support.

### H13. Verifiable parental consent and the DPDP notice (DATA-14, DATA-08, high)

- **Provision.** DPDP s.9(1) as amended by [S.O. 5458(E)][dpdp-order]: "before processing any
  personal data of a child ... obtain verifiable consent of the parent"; Rule 10(1): due diligence
  that the parent is "an adult who is identifiable", by reliable details already held, or details
  "voluntarily provided ... by the individual; or ... through a virtual token ... issued by an
  authorised entity" (Illustration Case 4 points to government-issued identity or a DigiLocker
  token). s.5(1), s.6(3), Rule 3: an itemised notice, purposes, rights, how to complain to the
  Board, in English or an Eighth Schedule language. From 13 May 2027.
- **Why it applies.** Kheelu exists to process the speech of 3 to 7 year olds; no Fourth Schedule
  exemption covers a toy maker. /safety already promises "Nothing is collected without your
  consent" with no flow described.
- **Founder action.** Build the consent step into parent-app setup before the first unit ships
  (AI mode off until consent is recorded; keep proof, s.6(10)). Choose the adult check with
  counsel. Write each consent notice as structured content (one item, one purpose) so it can be
  translated; counsel to rule on how many Eighth Schedule languages must be offered.

### H14. Summaries, alerts and the new-word count may be "behavioural monitoring" (DATA-15, high)

- **Provision.** DPDP s.9(3): "A Data Fiduciary shall not undertake tracking or behavioural
  monitoring of children or targeted advertising directed at children." Fourth Schedule
  exemptions cover schools, crèches and their transport, and real-time location for safety; none
  covers conversation analysis. From 13 May 2027; Schedule item 3 penalty up to ₹200 crore.
- **Why it applies.** The site sells a daily summary, alerts, a new-word count, memory of where the
  child stopped, and a verbatim log.
- **Founder action.** Get counsel's written view feature by feature before 13 May 2027; publish
  only features it clears; avoid "monitor", "track" and "report on your child".

### H15. Retention: 36 months versus purpose, and one-year logs versus "delete in one tap" (DATA-20, DATA-21, high)

- **Provision.** DPDP s.8(7)(a): erase "as soon as it is reasonable to assume that the specified
  purpose is no longer being served"; Rule 8(3): keep personal data, traffic data and logs "for a
  minimum period of one year from the date of such processing"; Rule 6(1)(e) (one-year logs).
- **Why it applies.** D020: transcripts kept 36 months; parents delete in one tap; providers keep
  nothing. If the parent app lapses with Kheelona+ after 6 months, 36 months may outlast the
  purpose; and from 13 May 2027 a one-tap delete can remove a conversation from view at once but
  not from every system.
- **Founder action.** Settle F7 first. Retire "Nothing is kept that you cannot delete". Counsel to
  approve a delete line such as "Removed from your app at once, and erased from our systems after
  the minimum period the law sets."

### H16. Tracking runs with no consent and no opt-out (DATA-11, high)

- **Provision.** DPDP s.4(1): personal data may be processed only "(a) for which the Data Principal
  has given her consent; or (b) for certain legitimate uses"; s.6(4): withdrawal "with the ease
  ... comparable to the ease with which such consent was given". SPDI Rule 5(7) (an option not to
  provide information; its reach to non-sensitive tracking data is a counsel point).
- **Why it applies.** All tools load on first view; /privacy says the proxied PostHog and the
  server report to Meta cannot be blocked. Analytics and ad measurement are not s.7 legitimate uses.
- **Founder action.** Decide at Gate A ([G6](../open-questions.md)) how a consent gate ships; it
  must be live by 13 May 2027. One consent state must switch every tag and both server routes.
