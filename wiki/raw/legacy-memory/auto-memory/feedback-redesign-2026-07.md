Founder feedback on the shipped kheelona.com design (2026-07-08): "it feels like we have forcefully put together various sections. It does not look like one single flowing smooth website." Mandate: ground-up redesign, completely immersive, browser-native interactive 3D (WebGL/R3F), not a flat video background. Content/copy stays. This REVERSES "2D everywhere" (2026-07-08) and "3D stays SUBTLE" (2026-07-06) with founder authority.

Key nuance: the founder LIKES the Storybook direction (Concept A's soul); what's rejected is the execution — flat washes and hard-joined stacked sections. "Rejected direction" ≠ "rejected feel".

**Why:** the founder judges designs by flow and immersion, not by section-level polish; seams between sections read as failure even when each section is good.

**How to apply:** design as one continuous spatial experience (camera journeys, wash gradients, page-turn metaphors), never stacked blocks. When the founder rejects something, ask/verify whether it's the concept or the execution before discarding the lane. Redesign plan: [[project-kheelona-com-build-prompt]], asset rules: [[gemini-asset-pipeline]].

Round-2 outcomes (2026-07-09): founder picked Direction 1 (Lumi's World) + "some things from" Direction 4, delegating the blend. Of the D4 elements, pop-up entrances and paper-craft scenery survived; the **bookmark-ribbon chapter nav was rejected on sight** ("does not look good") — fixed colored-chrome overlays read as clutter to this founder; keep navigation minimal, let the world carry the wow. Founder ships fast: approved the first 3D build for temp-live public feedback same day.

**R5 calm pullback (2026-07-10, reviewing the live R4 journey):** "Too much going on... looks like a class 5 student did it. Main failure points are 3D artifacts — they fly, they come and go. Not able to tell a story." The founder's immersion ceiling is LOW-MOTION: the /playos ambient room was the approved register ("more acceptable than homepage"). Mandated: 2D mascot + 2D Lumi (3D kept one flip away), fewer/fainter shapes never over text, smooth gradients (no color breaks), zero italics, ALL text left-aligned, white button/band text, 80/20 primary/secondary color, boxed feelings cards, sticky pre-order CTA always visible, 100% cross-page consistency. Pattern across the arc: the founder wants the storybook WORLD but repeatedly strips anything that moves attention away from the copy — bias every future visual call toward stillness and polish over spectacle.
