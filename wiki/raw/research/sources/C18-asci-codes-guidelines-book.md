# ASCI Codes and Guidelines book

- **Source:** Advertising Standards Council of India, "ASCI Codes and Guidelines" (the Code for Self-Regulation plus all sector guidelines), publisher's own PDF, 78 pages (PDF created 23 August 2025). Linked from ASCI's Code page.
- **URL:** https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/guidelines/ASCI_Codes_Guidelines_Book.pdf
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10 local (2026-10-11 UTC), plain GET, HTTP 200, `application/pdf`. Read the Code's Chapters I and III and the education guidelines in full.

## Exact passages

The Code, Chapter I, Truthful and honest representation (PDF page 18):

> "1.1. Advertisements must be truthful. All descriptions, claims and comparisons, which relate to matters of objectively ascertainable fact, should be capable of substantiation. Advertisers and advertising agencies are required to produce such substantiation as and when called upon to do so by The Advertising Standards Council of India."

> "1.2. Where advertising claims are expressly stated to be based on, or supported by independent research or assessment, its source and date should be indicated in the advertisement."

The Code, Chapter III (PDF page 22):

> "3.2. Advertisements addressed to children shall not contain anything, whether in illustration or otherwise, which might result in their physical, mental or moral harm, or which exploits their vulnerability."

"ASCI Guidelines for Advertising of Educational Institutions, Programmes and Platforms", Preamble (PDF page 33):

> "Educational institutions such as universities, colleges, schools, coaching classes, Edtech platforms etc., which offer education and training programmes, play a vital role in building the intellectual capital of India."

> "The advertising of these products and services can have an impact on the minds of growing children and vulnerable parents."

Guideline 8 and the signature block (PDF page 35):

> "8. An advertisement must not create a false sense of urgency or fear of missing out that could accentuate anxieties amongst school students, or their parents."

> "Chairman Board of Governors, ASCI March 9th 2023"

## Bearing on C18

- "ASCI requires claims to be substantiated": supported by Code 1.1, for claims about "matters of objectively ascertainable fact". Code 1.2 also asks that a claim said to rest on independent research give its source and date, which matches the D026 marker rule.
- "bars ads that play on parents' anxiety": the final rule is narrower. It bars creating "a false sense of urgency or fear of missing out that could accentuate anxieties amongst school students, or their parents".
- Scope: the education guidelines name educational institutions, coaching classes and EdTech platforms. Whether they bind a learning toy is not stated; the Code (Chapters I and III) applies to all advertising.
