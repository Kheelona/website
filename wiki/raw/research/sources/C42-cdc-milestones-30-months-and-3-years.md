# CDC, "Learn the Signs. Act Early." milestone pages

- **Source:** US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, "Learn the Signs. Act Early." milestone pages for 2 years, 30 months and 3 years. Each page shows "Reviewed: May 16, 2026". Government source; the milestones come from the AAP/CDC revision published as Zubler et al. (2022), see [C42-zubler-2022-pediatrics-milestones.md](C42-zubler-2022-pediatrics-milestones.md).

## Exact passages

30 months, Language/Communication:

> "Says about 50 words"

> "Says two or more words together, with one action word, like "Doggie run""

3 years, Language/Communication:

> "Talks with you in conversation using at least two back-and-forth exchanges"

> "Talks well enough for others to understand, most of the time"

Acting early (both pages, returned in two parts):

> "If your child is not meeting one or more milestones, has lost skills he or she once had"

> "or you have other concerns, act early."

> "Talk with your child's doctor, share your concerns, and ask about developmental screening."

## Bearing on C42

Supports three of the live sentence's four signs, with sharper wording: by 30 months most children say about 50 words; by 3 most talk well enough for **others** (not only family) to understand most of the time; losing skills at any point is a reason to act early. The action is "Talk with your child's doctor". These are US milestones set at the age most (75% or more) children reach them; missing one is a reason to ask, not a diagnosis.
