# Ruggeri, Lombrozo, Griffiths and Xu (2016), *Developmental Psychology* 52(12):2159-2173

- **Citation:** Ruggeri A, Lombrozo T, Griffiths TL, Xu F. "Sources of Developmental Change in the
  Efficiency of Information Search." *Developmental Psychology*, 2016; 52(12):2159-2173.
  DOI 10.1037/dev0000240. (Fei Xu, UC Berkeley; not Ying Xu.)
- **Role:** SECONDARY for C13. This is the paper behind the warning in SKM's search that "another
  paper cites Chouinard for over 100 questions per hour". Read to record the conflicting figure.
- **Read:** the publisher PDF hosted on the Princeton Computational Cognitive Science lab site,
  fetched 2026-10-10 by plain GET, no login.

## Exact passage

**Locator: p. 2159, introduction, right-hand column**
> "When engaged in conversations with adults, preschool-aged children ask on average over 100
> questions per hour (Chouinard, 2007)."

## Verifier's note

- This figure is for questions in general, not the information-seeking subset. It does not
  contradict "76 information-seeking questions an hour"; it does contradict SKM's pdf p.7 wording
  "Preschoolers ask about 76 questions an hour", which drops "information-seeking".
