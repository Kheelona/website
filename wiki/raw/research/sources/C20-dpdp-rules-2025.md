# Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025

- **Source:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Notification G.S.R. 846(E), New Delhi, 13 November 2025, The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3(i). Official text, hosted by MeitY (41 pages, Hindi then English).
- **URL:** https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf (the URL listed in PIB's 14 November 2025 backgrounder)
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10 local (2026-10-11 UTC), plain GET, HTTP 200, `application/pdf`. Read rule 1, rule 10, rule 12 and the Fourth Schedule (English).

## Exact passages

Rule 1, commencement (PDF page 24):

> "(4) Rules 3, 5 to 16, 22 and 23 shall come into force eighteen months after the date of publication of this Gazette."

Rule 10 (PDF page 27):

Heading: "Verifiable consent for processing of personal data of child."

> "(1) A Data Fiduciary shall adopt appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure that verifiable consent of the parent is obtained before the processing of any personal data of a child"

(The quote stops before the rest of the sub-rule, which sets the due-diligence check on the parent's identity and age.)

Fourth Schedule, Part A, item 3 (PDF page 37):

> "A Data Fiduciary who is an educational institution. | Processing is restricted to tracking and behavioural monitoring [...] (a) for the educational activities of such institution; or (b) in the interests of safety of children enrolled with such institution."

Fourth Schedule, Part B, item 4 (PDF page 37):

> "For the determination of real-time location of a child. | Processing is restricted to the tracking of real-time location of such child, in the interest of her safety and protection or security."

(The `|` marks the column break between the schedule's two columns; `[...]` marks one omitted punctuation mark.)

## Bearing on C20

The child-consent rule (rule 10) starts on the same 18-month clock as section 9. The Fourth Schedule lists exemptions from section 9(1) and 9(3); a consumer toy maker is not one of the listed classes.
