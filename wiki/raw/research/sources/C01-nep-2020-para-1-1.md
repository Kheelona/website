## Exact passage

**Locator:** Part I. School Education, Chapter 1 "Early Childhood Care and Education: The Foundation
of Learning", paragraph 1.1. Printed page 7 (PDF page 8).

> "1.1. Over 85% of a child’s cumulative brain development occurs prior to the age of 6, indicating
> the critical importance of appropriate care and stimulation of the brain in the early years in
> order to ensure healthy brain development and growth."

## What this passage does and does not say

- It says **"cumulative brain development"** and **"prior to the age of 6"** (before 6). It does not
  say "85% of a child's brain develops" (SKM docx L67), which reads as 85% of the organ.
- The policy gives **no citation** for the 85% figure, here or in the rest of the document (no
  reference list). It is a policy statement, not a research finding.
- Two other official documents repeat it, also without a specific citation:
  - NCF for Foundational Stage 2022, section 1.1.1, p.15: "Research from neuroscience informs us that
    over 85% of an individual’s brain development occurs by the age of 6" (see
    [C05-ncf-fs-2022-brain-development.md](C05-ncf-fs-2022-brain-development.md)).
  - NIPUN Bharat (PIB, 2021), PDF p.16 slide heading: "85% of a child's brain develops before the age
    of 6" (see [C04-nipun-bharat-2021.md](C04-nipun-bharat-2021.md)).
- The Harvard Center on the Developing Child pages that NCF-FS cites for its neuroscience (InBrief,
  Brain Architecture, read 2026-10-10) give **no** 85% figure.
- Nothing here mentions toys, devices or conversation.
