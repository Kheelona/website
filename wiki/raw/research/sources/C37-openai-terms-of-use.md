# OpenAI: Terms of Use (rest of world, which covers India)

- **Publisher:** OpenAI (primary; the provider's own terms for ChatGPT and its other services).
- **URL read:** https://openai.com/policies/row-terms-of-use/ (curl got HTTP 403; opened by GET in a
  real Chrome tab, no login; the tab landed on the en-GB copy of the same page,
  https://openai.com/en-GB/policies/row-terms-of-use/).
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10.
- **Effective date on the page:** "Effective: January 1, 2026".

## Exact passage

**Locator: "Registration and access", "Minimum age."**
> "You must be at least 13 years old or the minimum age required in your country to consent to use
> the Services. If you are under 18 you must have your parent or legal guardian’s permission to use
> the Services."

## What it does and does not say

- Minimum age **13** (or higher where local law requires), with parental permission under 18.
- It does not say ChatGPT is "built for adults": it admits users from 13.
