# C33 companion: the AAP 2016 policy statement for ages 5 to 18

**Why it is here.** The wiki draft (research/keyword-map.md L550) says the AAP, "from 6, ... asks
families to set consistent limits instead of a fixed number". That advice is NOT in "Media and Young
Minds" (ages 0 to 5). It is in this companion statement and in the AAP news release of 2016-10-21
(see `C33-aap-2016-news-release.md` in this folder).

**Source.** American Academy of Pediatrics, Council on Communications and Media. "Media Use in
School-Aged Children and Adolescents." Policy statement. *Pediatrics* 2016 Nov;138(5):e20162592.
DOI 10.1542/peds.2016-2592. PMID 27940794.

**Read.** The publisher's full-text page as archived by the Internet Archive on 2019-12-15
(https://web.archive.org/web/20191215072039/https://pediatrics.aappublications.org/content/138/5/e20162592),
fetched 2026-10-10. Abstract cross-checked the same day via NCBI E-utilities efetch for PMID
27940794. The live publications.aap.org page was not tried after the 0 to 5 statement's page
returned a 403 bot challenge.

## Exact passages

Abstract:
> "This policy statement focuses on children and adolescents 5 through 18 years of age."

> "In this new era, evidence regarding healthy media use does not support a one-size-fits-all
> approach."

"Recommendations", "Families":
> "Place consistent limits on hours per day of media use as well as types of media used."

## Notes for copy

- The statement's band is 5 through 18. "From 6" comes from the AAP news release, not from this
  statement.
- It gives no fixed number of hours; "instead of a fixed number" is a fair summary of "does not
  support a one-size-fits-all approach" plus "consistent limits".
