**Locator: quote from Robbie Torney, Head of AI & Digital Assessments**
> "More than a quarter of outputs included inappropriate content such as mentions of self-harm,
> drugs, and risky behaviors."

## What this page does and does not say

- The advice is about **AI toys**, defined as "smart toys with voice-based interactions", not only
  toys marketed as companions. A talking AI toy for ages 3 and up is inside that definition.
- It gives **four** findings with equal weight: attachment by design, inappropriate outputs (27%),
  data collection, unreliability. It does not say the advice is "mainly" about attachment.
- The upper band is **6 to 12** ("6–12" in the subheading).
