## Exact passages

**Locator: paragraph 1 and the two bill bullets**
> "On September 10, California Governor Gavin Newsom signed AI companion chatbot bills SB 867 and SB
> 1119 into law."

> "SB 867 imposes a four-year moratorium on the manufacture or sale of a toy designed for children
> under 16 years of age that contains an AI companion chatbot. The moratorium will commence January
> 1, 2027 and last until January 1, 2031."

> "SB 1119 is a broader measure that establishes requirements for all AI companion chatbots, with
> primary focus on those used by children under the age of 18."

**Locator: closing paragraph (its own position)**
