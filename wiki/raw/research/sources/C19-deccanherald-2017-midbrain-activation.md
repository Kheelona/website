## Exact passages

> "Social activist and Federation of Indian Rationalist Association president Narendra Nayak alleged that the entity called ‘Smart Brain’, operating in Puttur, Sullia, Udupi, Murdeshwar and other parts of Karnataka, is a fraud."

> "Addressing reporters in Mangaluru on Saturday, he said that the ‘Smart Brain’ has been running a racket called as WBDP (Whole Brain Development Programme), which they claim to balance the left and rights sides of brain but is totally unscientific and non-implementable."

> "The children in the age group of 6 to 16 years are being made the targets."

> "The children are also made to read texts blindfolded. The blindfolded reading is a simple trick which could be played by anyone."

## Bearing on C19

A second dated record, two years after The Hindu's, that a rationalist publicly called such a class a fraud. Note that the scheme was sold as a "Whole Brain Development Programme", which is close to the words "brain development" in our own category. These are allegations reported by a newspaper, not findings by a court or regulator.
