## Exact passages

**Locator: "National Mission: NIPUN BHARAT Vision", PDF p.7.**
> "The vision of the Mission is to create an enabling environment to ensure universal acquisition of
> foundational literacy and numeracy, so that by 2026-27 every child achieves the desired learning
> competencies in reading, writing and numeracy at the end of Grade III and not later than Grade V."

**Locator: "Lakshyas: Learning Goals of the Mission", PDF p.11.**
> "The overall literacy and numeracy targets to achieve the objectives of the Mission are set in the
> form of Lakshya or Targets for Foundational Literacy and Numeracy starting from the Balvatika."

Grade 3 column of the Lakshya diagram, PDF p.11 (bullets as printed):
> "Read with meaning" / "at least 60 words per minute" / "Read and write numbers up to 9999" /
> "Solve simple multiplication problems"

Grade 2 column (bullets as printed):
> "Read with meaning" / "45-60 words per minute" / "Read and write numbers up to 999" / "Subtract
> numbers up to 99"

**Locator: "School Preparation Module" slide heading, PDF p.16.**
> "85% of a child's brain develops before the age of 6"

## What these passages do and do not say

- The mission's goals run **from Balvatika up to Grade 3**, with a 2026-27 target. SKM's "school-year
  goals up to Grade 3 (NIPUN Bharat)" matches.
- Multiplication first appears in the **Grade 3** column ("Solve simple multiplication problems").
  Under NEP 2020's structure Grade 3 starts the Preparatory Stage at about age 8. Nothing here sets a
  times-tables goal at ages 6 to 7.
- The p.16 heading repeats the NEP figure in looser words, with no citation.
- Nothing here mentions toys, devices or Kheelu.
