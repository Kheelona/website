## Exact passages

Table 1, criteria:

> "Milestones are included at the age most (≥75%) children would be expected to demonstrate the milestone"

Milestone table rows (milestone | age); the first three are in Table 4, Language and Communication Milestones, the 2-step item is in Table 5, Cognitive Milestones:

> "Says ~50 words | 30 mo"

> "Talks well enough for others to understand, most of the time | 3 y"

> "Follows 1-step directions without any gestures, like giving you the toy when you say, “Give it to me.” | 18 mo"

> "Follows 2-step instructions, for example, “Put the toy down and close the door.” | 30 mo"

## Bearing on C42

Gives a source for each sign in the live line: "very few words by two and a half" (most say about 50 at 30 months), "hard to understand at three" (most are understood by others most of the time at 3), and "seems not to understand simple things you say" (most follow a one-step direction without gestures by 18 months and a two-step one by 30 months). Losing skills is covered by the CDC act-early message.
