# NCF for Foundational Stage 2022: the stage is ages 3 to 8

- **Document:** National Curriculum Framework for Foundational Stage 2022. Title page: "National
  Steering Committee for National Curriculum Frameworks". Foreword addressed to the Minister of
  Education, Government of India. 360 pages; PDF metadata created 2022-10-19.

## Exact passages

**Locator: Foreword (1.1), p.5.**
> "This is the first ever integrated Curriculum Framework for children between ages 3-8 in India. It
> is a direct outcome of the 5+3+3+4 ‘curricular and pedagogical’ structure that NEP 2020 has come
> out with for School Education."

**Locator: Chapter 1, section 1.1.2 "Foundational Stage", sub-section d, p.16.**
> "Considering all of the above, NEP 2020 has articulated the age range of 3-8 as the Foundational
> Stage, in the new 5+3+3+4 system."

**Locator: section 1.1.2 b "In institutional settings: Ages 3-8", p.16.**
> "i. 3-6 years: Early childhood education programmes in Anganwadis, Balvatikas, or pre schools.
> ii. 6-8 years: Early primary education programmes in school (Grades 1 and 2)."

## What these passages do and do not say

- A second official document confirms NEP 2020 sets the **Foundational Stage at ages 3 to 8**.
- The stage is defined by **institutional setting** (pre-school 3 to 6, Grades 1 and 2 at 6 to 8).
