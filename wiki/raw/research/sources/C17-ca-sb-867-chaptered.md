## Exact passages

**Locator: SEC. 1, Business and Professions Code section 22601(b)(1)**
> "“Companion chatbot” means an artificial intelligence system with a natural language interface
> that provides adaptive, human-like responses to user inputs and is capable of meeting a user’s
> social needs, including by exhibiting anthropomorphic features and being able to sustain a
> relationship across multiple interactions."

**Locator: SEC. 1, section 22601(b)(2)(C) (one of three exclusions)**
> "A stand-alone consumer electronic device that functions as a speaker and voice command
> interface, acts as a voice-activated virtual assistant, and does not sustain a relationship across
> multiple interactions or generate outputs that are likely to elicit emotional responses in the
> user."

**Locator: SEC. 1, section 22601(g)**
> "“Toy” means a physical product designed, marketed, or manufactured for use in play by children
> under 16 years of age."

**Locator: SEC. 2, section 22604.5(a) and (b)**
> "(a) No person shall manufacture, sell, or exchange, possess with intent to sell or exchange, or
> expose or offer for sale or exchange to any retailer any toy that includes a companion chatbot.
> (b) This section shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2031, and as of that date is
> repealed."

## When it starts (not in the bill; from the California Constitution)

The bill text gives an end date but **no start date**, and its digest shows "Vote: majority", so it
is not an urgency statute. The default rule applies. California Constitution, Article IV, section
8(c)(1), read 2026-10-10 on leginfo by GET in the same browser tab:
> "a statute enacted at a regular session shall go into effect on January 1 next following a 90-day
> period from the date of enactment of the statute"

Enacted 10 September 2026; 90 days later is 9 December 2026; the next 1 January is **1 January
2027**. The Toy Association's summary says the same (C17 Toy Association excerpt).

## Earlier versions (why secondary sources disagree on the age)

The Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection analysis (hearing June 16, 2026, "As
Amended May 14, 2026", PDF read 2026-10-10, HTTP 200, https://apcp.assembly.ca.gov/media/1168) gives
the definition then in the bill:
> "Defines “toy” as a product designed or intended by the manufacturer for use in play by children
> under 18 years of age."

The enacted text says **under 16**. Reports quoting "under 18" are describing the May 2026 version.

## What the law does and does not say

- It bars making or selling, **in California**, any toy for children under 16 that "includes a
  companion chatbot", until 1 January 2031. It is a temporary prohibition, not a permanent ban.
- Whether a product is caught depends on what its AI **does** (the functional definition above),
  not on how it is marketed. Nothing in the text exempts a product because it is framed as
  educational or developmental. Whether any given toy meets the definition is a legal question.
