## Exact passages

**Locator: PDF p.3 [printed p.1]**
> "Thus, it is essential that young children's feelings get the same level of attention as their
> thinking."

**Locator: PDF p.4 [printed p.2]**
> "Associations between positive emotions and the availability of sensitive and responsive caregiving
> are strengthened during infancy in both behavior and brain architecture."

> "When feelings are not well managed, thinking can be impaired."

> "In terms of basic brain functioning, emotions support executive functions when they are well
> regulated but interfere with attention and decision-making when they are poorly controlled."

## What this paper does and does not say

- It ties emotion and thinking together and links positive emotions to **sensitive and responsive
  caregiving**.
- It does **not** say children learn "fastest" from exchanges that "feel good", and makes no claim
  about the speed of learning.
