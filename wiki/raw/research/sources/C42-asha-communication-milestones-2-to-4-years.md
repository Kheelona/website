# ASHA, Communication Milestones

- **Source:** American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA), public pages "Communication Milestones: 2 to 3 Years", "Communication Milestones: 3 to 4 Years" and "ASHA's Developmental Milestones: Birth to 5 Years". Professional body's own pages.
- **URLs:** https://www.asha.org/public/developmental-milestones/communication-milestones-2-to-3-years/, https://www.asha.org/public/developmental-milestones/communication-milestones-3-to-4-years/, https://www.asha.org/public/speech/development/chart/
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10 local (2026-10-11 UTC), plain GET, HTTP 200 each. Read the page text.

## Exact passages

2 to 3 years:

> "Speech is becoming clearer but may not be understandable to unfamiliar listeners or to people who do not know your child."

> "If your child does not meet many of the milestones within their age range, visit ASHA ProFind to find an ASHA-certified audiologist or speech-language pathologist (SLP) for an assessment."

3 to 4 years:

> "By age 4 years, your child speaks so that people can understand most of what they say."

Birth to 5 years page:

> "This is not a screening or diagnostic tool. It is meant to be used to start conversations with your child’s doctor and other professionals in seeking services or making a referral."

## Bearing on C42

Supports "talk to a speech-language professional" as a route, and that unfamiliar listeners may still struggle at 2 to 3 while family usually do not. ASHA frames it as "many of the milestones", not any single one.
