## Exact passage and locator

**Locator: second paragraph of the release.** It restates NEP para 4.11, beginning:

> "As envisaged in para 4.11 of National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, wherever possible"

and continues with the Grade 5 / Grade 8 and beyond wording (it says "is to be" where NEP says "will
be"). The first paragraph quotes Right to Education Act 2009, Chapter V, section 29(f) on the medium
of instruction being the child's mother tongue "as far as practicable".
