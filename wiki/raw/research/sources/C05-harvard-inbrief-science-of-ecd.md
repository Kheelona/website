## Exact passages

**Locator: PDF p.1, point 1 "Brains are built over time, from the bottom up."**
> "In the first few years of life, more than 1 million new neural connections are formed every
> second. After this period of rapid proliferation, connections are reduced through a process called
> pruning, so that brain circuits become more efficient."

**Locator: PDF p.2, point 3 "The brain’s capacity for change decreases with age."**
> "The brain is most flexible, or “plastic,” early in life to accommodate a wide range of
> environments and interactions, but as the maturing brain becomes more specialized to assume more
> complex functions, it is less capable of reorganizing and adapting to new or unexpected
> challenges."

**Locator: landing page summary and PDF p.1 lead (scope).** The lead says child development is a
foundation for society "particularly from birth to five years" (quoted fragment; the full sentence
contains em dashes).

## What this source does and does not say

- Same as the key concept page: the peak of new connections is **"the first few years of life"**,
  and the InBrief's own focus is **birth to five**. It does not say ages 3 to 6 are the fastest
  years.
- It gives **no** percentage of brain development by any age.
- Nothing here mentions toys or devices.
