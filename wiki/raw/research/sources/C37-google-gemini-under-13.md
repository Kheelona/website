# Google Gemini Apps Help: "Guide your child's Gemini Apps experience"

- **Publisher:** Google (primary; its own help centre).
- **URL read:** https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16109150?hl=en (HTTP 200).
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10, by plain GET (curl, browser user agent). No login. Found through a web
  search for Google's own help page; the page itself was read.

## Exact passages

**Locator: opening paragraph**
> "You can give your child under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) access to Gemini Apps.
> As a parent, you're in control of your child’s Gemini Apps access, including turning it on or off,
> using the Google Family Link app."

**Locator: opening paragraph, continued**
> "Like many other AI apps, Gemini is conversational and can feel like a human. It's important for
> you to help your child understand that it's an AI tool, not a person to confide in."

**Locator: "Gemini Apps availability for supervised accounts"**
> "Gemini Apps are not available for supervised accounts in the European Economic Area,
> Switzerland, and the United Kingdom."

## What it does and does not say

- Gemini is a counter-example to "the best-known chatbots set a minimum age of 13 or older": a parent
  can switch it on for a child **under 13** through Family Link (outside the EEA, Switzerland and
  the UK). Those are the only exclusions the page names; availability for supervised accounts in
  India specifically was not checked.
