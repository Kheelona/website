## Exact passage

**Locator: Abstract**
> "Families of 52 4-to-6 year-old children were randomly assigned to a 9-week, interactive,
> family-based intervention or no-contact control group. ... Families who completed the intervention
> exhibited greater increases in adult-child conversational turns, and changes in turn-taking mediated
> intervention effects on language and executive functioning measures. Collapsing across groups,
> turn-taking changes were also positively correlated with cortical thickening in left inferior
> frontal and supramarginal gyri, the latter of which mediated relationships between changes in
> turn-taking and children's language development."

## Use

- Still ages 4 to 6, still **adult-child** turns, and the intervention was for **families**. It does
  not test a toy. The brain link is "positively correlated" across groups.
