# National Education Policy 2020, the 5+3+3+4 structure and the Foundational Stage

- **Document, URL, fetch:** as in [C01-nep-2020-para-1-1.md](C01-nep-2020-para-1-1.md). Official full
  text hosted by NCERT, https://ncert.nic.in/pdf/nep/NEP_2020.pdf, read 2026-10-10 by plain GET.
  Ministry of Education copies answered 404/403 that day. SKM's EducationWorld link not relied on.

## Exact passages

**Locator: Part I. School Education, opening text under the structure diagram. Printed page 7 (PDF
page 8).**
> "Currently, children in the age group of 3-6 are not covered in the 10+2 structure as Class 1
> begins at age 6. In the new 5+3+3+4 structure, a strong base of Early Childhood Care and Education
> (ECCE) from age 3 is also included, which is aimed at promoting better overall learning,
> development, and well-being."

**Locator: Chapter 4 "Curriculum and Pedagogy in Schools", paragraph 4.1. Printed page 11 (PDF
page 12).**
> "4.1. The curricular and pedagogical structure of school education will be reconfigured to make it
> responsive and relevant to the developmental needs and interests of learners at different stages
> of their development, corresponding to the age ranges of 3-8, 8-11, 11-14, and 14-18 years,
> respectively. The curricular and pedagogical structure and the curricular framework for school
> education will therefore be guided by a 5+3+3+4 design, consisting of the Foundational Stage (in
> two parts, that is, 3 years of Anganwadi/pre-school + 2 years in primary school in Grades 1-2; both
> together covering ages 3-8), Preparatory Stage (Grades 3-5, covering ages 8-11), Middle Stage
> (Grades 6-8, covering ages 11-14), and Secondary Stage (Grades 9-12 in two phases, i.e., 9 and 10
> in the first and 11 and 12 in the second, covering ages 14-18)."

**Locator: paragraph 4.2, first sentence. Printed page 11 (PDF page 12).**
> "4.2. The Foundational Stage will consist of five years of flexible, multilevel, play/activity-based
> learning and the curriculum and pedagogy of ECCE as mentioned in para 1.2."

## What these passages do and do not say

- NEP's term is the **"Foundational Stage"**, a stage of the school structure: 3 years of
  Anganwadi/pre-school plus Grades 1 and 2, ages 3 to 8. "Foundational years" is SKM's paraphrase.
- It is a curricular grouping, not a statement that ages 3 to 8 are when the brain develops most.
- Corroborated by NCF for Foundational Stage 2022, section 1.1.2 d, p.16: "NEP 2020 has articulated
  the age range of 3-8 as the Foundational Stage, in the new 5+3+3+4 system." (see
  [C02-ncf-fs-2022-foundational-stage.md](C02-ncf-fs-2022-foundational-stage.md)).
