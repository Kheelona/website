# G.S.R. 843(E): which parts of the DPDP Act are in force, and when

- **Source:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Notification G.S.R. 843(E), New Delhi, 13 November 2025, The Gazette of India, Extraordinary, No. 757 (Gazette ID CG-DL-E-14112025-267647). Official text, hosted by MeitY. Bilingual (Hindi and English).
- **URL:** https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf
- **Found by:** a domain-restricted search of meity.gov.in, egazette.gov.in and pib.gov.in; law-firm summaries were seen in search results but not relied on.
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10 local (2026-10-11 UTC), plain GET, HTTP 200, `application/pdf`. Read the English notification in full (PDF page 2).

## Exact passages (English text, PDF page 2)

> "In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (2) of section 1 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (22 of 2023), the Central Government hereby appoints"

> "(a) the date of publication of this notification in the Official Gazette as the date on which the provisions of sub-section (2) of section 1, section 2, sections 18 to 26 sections 35, 38, 39, 40, 41, 42, 43, and sub-sections (1) and (3) of section 44 of the said Act shall come into force;"

> "(c) eighteen months from the date of publication of this gazette, on which the provision of sections 3 to 5, sub-sections (1) to (8) and (10) of section 6,sections 7 to 10, sections 11 to 17, section 27 except clause (d) of sub-section (1) of the said section, sections 28 to 34, 36, 37 and sub-section (2) of section 44 of the said Act shall come into force."

## Bearing on C20

Section 9 falls in tranche (c): it comes into force eighteen months after the Gazette of 13 November 2025, that is in May 2027 (by our arithmetic, about 13 May 2027; the notification gives the rule, not the calendar date). On 2026-10-10 section 9 is enacted but **not yet in force**. So the live line "already answers the last one for you" (stories.ts:144) overstates it; "the law says" or "from May 2027, the law requires" is accurate.

## Checked for later changes (2026-10-10)

- A domain-restricted search of meity.gov.in, egazette.gov.in and pib.gov.in found **no official notification shortening the 18-month period**. Secondary legal sites (not relied on for the date) report that MeitY consulted in January 2026 on cutting it to 12 months, and that no such amendment had been notified as of late September 2026.
- Secondary sources (TaxTMI's summary page, law-firm notes) report a "Digital Personal Data Protection (Removal of Difficulties) Order, 2026", S.O. 5458(E) dated 5 October 2026, correcting the wording of section 9(1) so it reads "a child or of a person with disability" and changing "audit" to "data audit" in section 10. **Its official text was not read** (TaxTMI shows only a summary behind a login; MeitY's pages render by script). It is not reported to change any commencement date. Re-check the Gazette before publishing any date.
