# Berkowitz et al. (2016), the authors' response to Frank

- **Source:** T. Berkowitz, M. W. Schaeffer, C. S. Rozek, E. A. Maloney, S. C. Levine, S. L. Beilock, "Response to Comment on 'Math at home adds up to achievement in school'", *Science* 351(6278): 1161, 11 March 2016. DOI 10.1126/science.aad8555. PMID 26965620.
- **What I could read:** the PubMed abstract only (NCBI E-utilities, plain GET, HTTP 200, 2026-10-10 local). The full response was not read.

## Exact passage (abstract)

> "Frank presents an alternative interpretation of our data, yet reports largely similar results to those in our original Report. A critical difference centers on how to interpret and test interaction effects. Frank finds no mistakes in our analyses. We stand by our original conclusions of meaningful effects of the Bedtime Learning Together (BLT) math app on children's math achievement."

## Bearing on C21

The authors stand by their result, so the honest description is "disputed", not "refuted".
