## Bearing on C42

- **Two-word item (30 months): confirmed.** Table 4 has it at 30 months with normative data from four
  sources, two screening tools and five clinical-opinion sources, and no footnote e. The CDC page words it
  "Says two or more words together, with one action word, like “Doggie run”"; the table has "Says ≥2 words,
  with 1 action word, like “Doggie run.”" (no "together"). Same item. The plain two-word item ("More milk")
  sits at 24 months.
- **50 words (30 months): confirmed again,** same row as the 2026-10-10 excerpt.
- **Sentence item (4 years): confirmed as an item, with weaker evidence.** "Says sentences with ≥4 words" is
  at 4 years, marked New, with no normative data ("—"), one screening tool (ASQ-3) and one clinical-opinion
  source (ASHA), and footnote e: the experts agreed that more research would be beneficial. It is one of the
  22 milestones (13.8%) so marked.
