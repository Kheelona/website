# California SB 1119 (Padilla), "Companion chatbots: children's safety" ("Adam's Law"), Chapter 190, Statutes of 2026

- **Publisher:** California Legislative Information (Legislative Counsel), the official bill text.

## Exact passages

**Locator: section 21810**
> "This chapter shall be known as “Adam’s Law.”"

**Locator: section 21810.5(a)**
> "“Child” means a natural person under 18 years of age."

**Locator: Legislative Counsel's Digest, operative paragraph**
> "This bill would require an operator, as defined, of a companion chatbot to, beginning July 1,
> 2027, before making a new or substantially modified companion chatbot available to users in the
> state, do various things with respect to child safety, including perform and document a
> comprehensive risk assessment related to the design, configuration, and operation of the
> companion chatbot with respect to minor users"

## What it does and does not say

- SB 1119 **regulates** companion chatbot operators for users under 18 (risk assessments,
  independent child safety audits, parental controls). It is **not** a ban, and it is not about toys
  specifically. The toy prohibition is SB 867 (separate excerpt).
