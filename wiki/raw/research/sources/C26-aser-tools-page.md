# ASER Centre, "ASER tools"

- **Source:** ASER Centre (Pratham), "ASER tools" web page. Publisher's own page.
- **URL:** https://asercentre.org/aser-tools/
- **Fetched:** 2026-10-10 local (2026-10-11 UTC), plain GET, HTTP 200. Read the introductory text.

## Exact passages

> "The Annual Status of Education Report (ASER) survey has been conducted since 2005. Driven by concerns about low levels of attainment in reading and mathematics across the elementary school years and beyond, ASER tools were designed to capture basic learning outcomes using a common assessment for all children irrespective of age or grade."

> "ASER assesses children’s foundational reading, arithmetic, and, in some years, English skills. The ASER tool is a progressive tool that has different levels. Each child is evaluated based on the highest level they can comfortably achieve. For reading, levels range from recognizing letters to reading a Grade 2-level story. Arithmetic assessment levels range from recognizing numbers to performing basic 3-digit by 1-digit division."

## Bearing on C26

Confirms the levelled, highest-level-reached design. See [C26-aser-2024-assessment-tasks.md](C26-aser-2024-assessment-tasks.md) for the age range (5 to 16) and the home-based procedure.
