# Google: Terms of Service, "Age requirements" (and the Generative AI Additional Terms)

- **Publisher:** Google (primary; the provider's own terms, which govern Gemini Apps).
- **URLs read (HTTP 200, plain GET with curl, browser user agent, no login, 2026-10-10):**
  - https://policies.google.com/terms?hl=en ("Effective July 30, 2026"; the copy served was the USA version)
  - https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai?hl=en

## Exact passages

**Locator: Google Terms of Service, "Age requirements"**
> "If you’re under the age required to manage your own Google Account, you must have your parent or
> legal guardian’s permission to use a Google Account."

**Locator: Generative AI Additional Terms of Service, "Age requirements"**
> "In addition to the “Age requirements” section in the Google Terms of Service, you will comply with
> any further age restrictions presented in connection with a Service."

## What it does and does not say

- Google's terms set **no fixed minimum age of 13**. A child below the age to manage their own
  account may use Google services with a parent's permission. For Gemini specifically, see the
  Gemini under-13 excerpt.
