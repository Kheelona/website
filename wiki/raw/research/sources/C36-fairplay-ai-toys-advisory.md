# Fairplay: "AI Toys are NOT Safe for Kids" advisory (November 2025)

- **Publisher:** Fairplay (primary; its own press release, advisory page and advisory PDF).
- **URLs read (all HTTP 200, plain GET with curl, browser user agent, no login, 2026-10-10):**
  - Press release: https://fairplayforkids.org/ai-toys-unsafe-for-kids-this-holiday-season-advisory-warns/
  - Advisory page: https://fairplayforkids.org/pf/aitoyadvisory/
  - Advisory PDF (November 2025 upload, 8 pages): https://fairplayforkids.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/AI-Toys-Advisory.pdf
    (the page also links a January 2026 re-upload at `/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/AI-Toys-Advisory.pdf`;
    a text diff shows only changes to the signatory list.)
- **Date:** press release "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Nov. 20, 2025".

## Exact passages

**Locator: press release, paragraph 1**
> "A first-of-its-kind advisory is warning parents not to buy AI toys for their children this
> holiday season. Led by the child advocacy group Fairplay, the advisory is signed by about 80
> experts and 80 organizations"

**Locator: advisory page, opening paragraph**
> "That’s why, 78 organizations and 80 experts in child development and technology’s impact on kids,
> strongly advise families not to buy AI toys for children this holiday season."

**Locator: advisory PDF, page 1, opening paragraph**
> "we, the undersigned advocates and experts in child development and technology’s impact on kids,
> strongly advise families not to buy AI toys for children this holiday season."

**Locator: advisory page, "What are AI Toys?"**
> "AI toys are chatbots embedded in everyday children’s toys, like plushies, dolls, action figures,
> and kids’ robots."

## What these pages do and do not say

- Date (November 2025, the release is 20 November), the publisher (Fairplay, "the child advocacy
  group"), and the advice (do not buy AI toys "this holiday season") all match the live journal.
- The count: 78 organisations plus 80 experts is **158** on the advisory page, so "more than 150
  experts and organisations" holds. The press release rounds it to "about 80 experts and 80
  organizations". The signatory list was edited in January 2026, so the figure is as of the page
  read on 2026-10-10.
- Fairplay draws **no age line**: its advice covers AI toys for all children, and it notes they are
  "marketed to children as young as infants". It is not a source for "a line at 5" (C16).
