# Kheelona

> Kheelona builds PlayOS — the AI operating system for toys, baby care and physical companions ("the Android for physical AI"). B2B only: toy OEMs, character-IP owners and nursery brands license PlayOS free, drop the Kheelona Magic Box module into their own shell, and ship a screen-free conversational AI product under their own brand in 4–6 weeks. The only prices on kheelona.ai are per-unit module prices: $10–50 for companions, $30–100 for baby care. One call to action: book a partnership call at https://kheelona.ai/partner. Contact: connect@kheelona.com. Consumer purchases live at https://kheelona.com, never here.

Key facts:
- Kheelona sells two things; one is free. PlayOS (voice-to-voice AI engine, safety filters, OTA updates, parent app, SDK, 10+ languages) is free to adopt. The Kheelona Magic Box — the palm-sized module that runs it (custom PCB, MCU, mic array, speaker) — is $10–100 per unit by spec.
- Companion module ($10–50/unit, for plush, pets, character toys): Talk & Respond $10 · Real Conversation mid-band, priced on the spec call · Sees & Understands $50.
- Baby-care module ($30–100/unit, for monitors, cribs, bassinets): Monitors & Alerts $30 · Soothes & Responds mid-band · Sleeps & Learns $100.
- Partners set retail and keep the hardware margin; premium content, language packs and advanced models are revenue-shared. Partner IP stays with the partner.
- COPPA / GDPR-K, region-pinned data residency (US/EU/India), one-tap delete, no ads, no data resale — built into PlayOS; every partner build inherits the stack.
- Proprietary sub-1B-parameter Voice SLM trained on consented, kid-safe conversations; offered as API credits from Q4 2026.
- Traction (June 2026): Lumi live with 10 families daily; Lori production-ready and taking partner orders; 1 school MOU; 5+ OEM design-partner conversations; backed by NVIDIA Inception, Karnataka Elevate, Founders Inc.
