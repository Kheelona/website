# Google autocomplete, India (2026-10-10)

**What this is.** Google's own query predictions for India, for 181 seeds across the brief's intents
(buy / compare / learn / gift) and ages (3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 3-7), plus brand, competitor, Hinglish and
Hindi seeds, and an a…z expansion of four core seeds (104 more requests). Every request answered 200.

**What it is not.** Autocomplete carries **no volume**. Order is Google's ranking, which mixes
popularity, freshness and locale; it is evidence that people type a phrase, not how many. Volumes
live only in the Keyword Planner and Search Console figures in
[legacy-gsc-keyword-planner-2026-09.md](legacy-gsc-keyword-planner-2026-09.md).

**Localisation.** `gl=in` and an Indian IP. Results are India-flavoured (Blinkit, Meesho, Flipkart,
Indiamart, "india", "under 500", "under ₹ 500") but still carry some global retailer noise
(Walmart, Kmart, Smyths, "nz", "australia"). The undocumented `google:suggestsubtypes` codes are
kept in the companion JSON only and are not interpreted.

**Method files** (scratchpad, not in the repo): `scripts/seeds.json`, `scripts/suggest.mjs`,
`out/google-suggest-seeds.json`, `out/google-suggest-alpha.json`.

## Observations (from this capture only)

1. **Single ages, not ranges.** Every age 3-7 completes as "for N year old (boy/girl)". "3 to 7" is not
   a phrase Google predicts: `learning toys for 3 to 7 year old` falls back to "3 to 4", "3 to 5"
   and single ages; `educational toys for 3-7 years` falls back to "3 years".
2. **Boy / girl** is the first modifier on almost every age and gift seed.
3. **"brain development toys" skews young.** Its completions run to 1 year old, 2 year old, 6 months
   baby, babies, 8 month old before reaching 3, 4 and 5. Ages 3-6 do exist ("brain development toys
   for 3 year old", "…for 4 year old", "…for 5 year old", "…for preschoolers", "…for lkg students").
4. **"talking toys" collides with school homework**: an NCERT Class 3 English chapter called "Talking
   Toys" ("class 3 question answer", "worksheet", "word meaning"). The a…z expansion is full of it.
   Cheap mimic toys (cactus, parrot, Talking Tom) fill most of the rest.
5. **AI-toy phrasing exists but is thin**: `ai toy for kids india` completes only to itself;
   `best ai toy for kids india` and `ai toy vs` return nothing; `ai toys that talk back`,
   `talking toy with ai`, `talking toy ai`, `screen free ai toys`, `screen free intelligent toys` appear.
6. **Safety questions are live**: are ai toys safe (for kids / children / babies), is ai safe for kids,
   is ai good for kids, plus named-app safety questions.
7. **Speech and language is a deep vein**: learning toys for 3 year old **with speech delay** (4th
   completion), toys for speech delay 3 / 4 / 5 year old, speech development toys for 3 / 4 year olds,
   how to improve speech in 3 year old, my 3 year old is not talking (properly / yet / in sentences).
8. **Gift seeds are price-capped and India-tagged**: "under 500", "under 200", "india", "blinkit",
   "instant delivery". Seasonal: diwali gift for kids, children's day gift.
9. **Brand**: `kheelona` → "kheelona", "kheelona india" only. `kheelu` is not recognised (completes to
   "kheel", a puffed-rice sweet). `kheelu toy` → "kheelu toys india". **`lumi ai toy` still completes**
   ("lumi ai toy", "lumi ai toy box"). `khilona ai` → "ai khilona".
10. **Competitors**: Miko is a real query family (price in india, review, worth it, mini vs 3, vs eilik);
    `toys like miko` and `miko alternative` are mostly noise (Genshin's Yae Miko). Yoto and tonies
    have India-specific queries (yoto player india price, toniebox price in india) though neither
    sells in India directly (see [competitor-pages-2026-10-10.md](competitor-pages-2026-10-10.md)).
11. **Hinglish and Hindi**: strong learn-intent demand: bacche ko bolna kaise sikhayen (with jaldi,
    english, 4 / 5 saal), bacchon ka screen time kitna hona chahiye, mobile ki aadat kaise chhudayen,
    बच्चों का दिमाग तेज करने के उपाय. Buy intent: bolne wala khilona (price, ka naam), baat karne wala
    khilona. `bacchon ke liye ai khilona` returns nothing.
