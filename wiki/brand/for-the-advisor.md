# For the advisor: the facts and rules to write Kheelona copy from

A page the founder can forward to the marketing advisor before more copy is written
([D061](../decisions/D061-advisor-proposal-what-we-take-2026-10-15.md)). The October draft was built partly on facts
that have since changed, so most of it could not be used as written. **This is a summary: the linked page is always
the source, and the latest founder decision wins.**

## Rules every line follows

- **The promise is conversation**, not the brain: "Kheelu answers every why. Then it asks one back." Brain
  development is a topic parents search for, never what Kheelu does ([D016](../decisions/D016-brain-wording.md)).
- **No invented claims**: every fact traces to [facts](../product/facts.md) or a founder decision; a statistic needs its
  original source ([source verification](../research/source-verification.md)). No "completely safe", no IQ, no
  "builds skills", no feelings or mood reading.
- **No wake word**: the microphone is on only after a press of the wake-up button
  ([D018](../decisions/D018-wake-up-button.md)).
- **Voice**: plain Indian English, the parent first, one idea per line, written for a phone
  ([voice](voice.md)). The look comes only from the design system, not new colours or fonts.
- **No scarcity tricks**: no counters, "only X left", or "ships soon" urgency.

The full list of banned and retired wording: [claims](../product/claims.md) §4 and `test/guards/banned-phrases.data.ts`.
